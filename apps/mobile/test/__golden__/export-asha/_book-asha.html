Early Letters

Letters to Asha

For Asha, from everyone who talked to you this year.

About this book

Every word in this book was spoken or typed by Asha's family. Nothing was reworded afterwards. The only changes were small fixes, like a misheard word or a stray "um", and each one could be undone. Each letter is signed by the person who wrote it, and the voices are kept too, so these pages can be heard as well as read. Letters appear in the language they were said, just as your family speaks.
