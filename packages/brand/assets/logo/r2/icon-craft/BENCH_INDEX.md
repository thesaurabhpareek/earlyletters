Generated by `node bench.mjs --batch` (do not edit by hand; notes live in BENCH_NOTES.md and are appended below).
Rules and what is verified: [ICON_RULES.md](ICON_RULES.md).

Every concept is composed the same way from its own `symbol.svg`: paper #FBF8F3 mark on sepia #8A5A3B (default), accentDark #D9A47E on warm near-black (dark), simulated tinted and clear. ## Bench notes (icon-craft, what I saw looking at the renders)

Bench limits, read before quoting any render: Liquid Glass is a static approximation (rim light only, no refraction or motion). Tinted and clear are simulated from the mark's alpha. The superellipse mask is close to, not identical to, Apple's. Neighbour icons are generic tiles that echo common App Store colour patterns, not real marks. Final sign-off belongs in Icon Composer or on a device.

### Calibration: round 1 (`r1-a`, `r1-b`)
- **r1-a (serif e with bar):** strongest mass on the home screen; holds at 16 px. The crossbar gap survives at 29 px but becomes a 1 px line, which is exactly the strikethrough reading the brief bans. Bench agrees with round 1 lessons.
- **r1-b (monoline e with wave):** the thinnest object on any of the four home screens. At 29 px the line is about 1 px wide and greys out; at 16 px it is a smudge. Beside neighbours whose glyphs are solid masses, it looks unfinished. Confirms HIG "avoid extremely thin line weights" and the "rope, not pen" lesson.
- Calibration conclusion: the bench separates a mass mark from a line mark the way a phone does. A mark needs its thinnest essential part at about 56 px or more on the 1024 master (see ICON_RULES.md rule 25).

### two-voices (v1)
- **Holds:** two solid teardrop masses carry the icon at every size; reads at 16 px as two blobs, at 29 px clearly as two marks. Dark, tinted and clear all keep it legible because it is pure silhouette. Among the strongest silhouettes on the sheet.
- **Breaks:** the big mark's arching arm and the small mark's tail thin to under 1 px at 29 px (see `sizes.png`, 8x row) so the "bending toward" gesture, the whole idea, disappears below 40 px. What remains is two blobs.
- **Misreads at home-screen size (my eye, for the critics to test):** a pair of cherries, a musical note pair, a comma pair / quote glyph (which lands near Medium, Quora-style quote marks and many podcast and messaging icons), and in tinted mode a "66".
- **Production:** submitted `app-icon-1024.png` is opaque RGB (passes). Designer's placement is larger than the bench default; both read. The small cut thickens the tails slightly and helps at 29 px; it only ships through an asset catalog, not a `.icon` file.
- Suggested fix: thicken the arm and tail on the master to at least 56 px, or accept that the small-size read is "two marks" and make sure that alone still says something.

### moon-letter (v1)
- **Holds:** the cleanest small-size read on the bench so far. Two filled shapes, a crescent above an envelope body, no hairlines. At 16 px it is still "a crescent over a box"; at 29 px the V notch survives. Dark, tinted and clear keep it because it is pure silhouette. Sits comfortably among system icons; feels calm rather than babyish.
- **Breaks:** the notch between moon and envelope is the only thing that says "letter", and at 16 to 29 px the envelope reads as a tray. The crescent's inner tip goes to about 1 px at 29 px.
- **Misreads / collisions at home-screen size:** a generic "Inbox" or "Mail tray" glyph; a sleep app (moons are the category cliche: see the bench's own "Baby Log" moon neighbour, one row below, which competes directly); in tinted mode it is one of three moons on my mock screen. Night-mode or Do Not Disturb association (the iOS Focus moon).
- **Production:** opaque RGB 1024 submitted. The small cut is a modest gain (thicker moon tip) and only ships through an asset catalog.
- Suggested fix: give the envelope one more cue that survives 29 px (a visible flap edge or a heavier notch), so it is not a tray.

### held (v1)
- **Holds:** big, bright, wide silhouette; the open book is instantly legible at 60 pt in every appearance.
- **Breaks:** the mark is wide and short (76 x 41 percent of the canvas at bench size), so it has the least vertical mass on the screen. The book's gutter is a hairline gap that disappears at 29 px, and the small card becomes a single dot at 16 px. Below 40 px it is "a bright W".
- **Misreads:** Apple Books' family and every reading app (the Books tile sits two rows down on my mock and they rhyme); a letter M or W; a bird in flight; mountains/hills; at 29 px a crown. The card resting on the page can read as a falling or tipping object.
- **Production:** submitted `app-icon-1024.png` is opaque RGB (passes); see `submitted-vs-bench.png`.
- Suggested fix: widen the gutter to at least 56 px on the master, and enlarge the card so it is a clear second object at 29 px, or drop it and let the cradle carry the meaning.

### kept-note (v1)
- **Holds:** the sturdiest small-size performer with moon-letter. A tall paper block with a heart knocked out of it; at 16 px it is still "a white card with a dark notch", at 29 px the heart is clear. Pure silhouette, so dark, tinted and clear are all solid. Tall proportion (50 x 63 percent) suits the square well.
- **Breaks:** the fold gap and the turning right leaf are about 1 px at 29 px; below 40 px the "opened a little" gesture is gone and it is a card with a heart. The heart cut into the fold has a stem (the gap) running down from its point; at small sizes that reads as a heart on a stick, or a keyhole.
- **Misreads:** a door or wardrobe with a heart cut-out (the outhouse-door heart is a known folk motif, worth a culture check); a greeting card / Valentine card app; a playing card (hearts); a dating app; a medical or donor card. In tinted mode the heart is the only detail and the mark becomes "a heart app".
- **Production:** opaque RGB 1024 submitted. The small cut widens the fold and heart a little and helps at 16 px.
- Suggested fix: make sure the heart stays an emergent negative (brief rule) and does not dominate; consider closing the stem below the heart so it is not a keyhole.

### wildcard (v1)
- **Holds:** distinctive; nothing else on the screen looks like it. The three discs survive at every size as three dots of falling size.
- **Breaks:** the arms and hanger are hairlines. At 60 pt on the home screen they are faint; at 29 px they are about 1 px and grey; at 16 px only three dots remain. The mark sits in the top half of the tile with empty space under it, so it looks the lightest and smallest icon on the screen (see `sheet-home.png`). This is the HIG "extremely thin line weights" failure, same family as round 1 B.
- **Misreads:** at small size three dots read as a loading indicator, a molecule, a "more" glyph or music notes; larger, a balance scale or a coat rack. The designer already flags org chart and molecule.
- **Production:** opaque RGB 1024 submitted. The heavier small cut helps arms only slightly.
- Suggested fix: arms at least 56 px on the master, bigger discs, and centre the whole mobile optically (it is visually top-heavy in the square).

### years (v1)
- **Holds:** compact, centred, strong mass (56 x 56 percent); concentric rings survive to 29 px, and the bold ring silhouette is one of the most visible icons on the sheet in every appearance.
- **Breaks:** the inner seed and ring gaps merge at 16 px into a single blob with a hole. The "resting on one ground point" idea is invisible at icon size.
- **Misreads:** an eye (designer flags it), a target / bullseye, a snail shell, a camera lens or shutter, an ear, a whirlpool. On my mock screen it rhymes with the Podcasts-style ring tile and the Fitness-style ring tile, and in tinted mode it is one of three rings. An eye is a surveillance reading for a private family archive: the worst possible misread for this product.
- **Production:** opaque RGB 1024 submitted.

### All six side by side: `bench/COMPARE_V1.png`
Small-size robustness only (the icon's job, not the whole brand decision), best first, from the 29 px and 16 px columns:

3. **two-voices**: two masses survive; the gesture between them does not.
