# Rejected or superseded explorations

- `round1-wordmark.png`: round 1 (Literata 500 at text optical size). Sturdy but stubby wedge serifs, font kerning, a blunt rectangle bridging the tt. Superseded by route A.
- `alternative-serifs-specimen.png`: Literata at three optical sizes against Newsreader, Source Serif 4, EB Garamond and Fraunces. Newsreader reads sharper and colder ("newspaper"); Source Serif 4 is neutral to the point of anonymity; Fraunces is warm but was the default startup serif of 2021 to 2024. Only EB Garamond earned a full route (D).
- `optical-size-study.png`: Literata at opsz 12, 24, 36, 48, 72. 12 is too stocky for a logo, 72 too fragile for anything below 64 px. 36 at weight 480 chosen for route A.
- `tt-height-study.png`: first t raised 0, 48 and 95 units, and the second t raised instead. 95 reads as a mistake ("l"); raising the second t is the historical Caslon habit and reads as decoration, not as a tall and a small letter. 48 on the first t kept.
