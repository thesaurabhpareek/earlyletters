Direction A, the letter. On paper
At night

Literata e, SemiBold, opened along the centre of its crossbar. The two halves part by 22 units on a 1000 unit em, so each half of the bar keeps its weight. The opening widens in the icon cut (42) and the small cut (46) so the fold survives on a phone and in a browser tab.

Rationale

Folded once, opened for years

The symbol is a lowercase e folded once along its crossbar, the same crease a letter gets when it goes into an envelope and the one it keeps after it has been opened again and again.

It is cut from Literata, the serif the letters are read in, so the mark, the wordmark and every letter in the book share one voice; the only invention is the fold.

At 16 pixels it is simply a sturdy e, on a book spine it is a quiet monogram, and it never borrows the stock language of envelopes, seals or quills.

Sizes

From browser tab to book cover

16 and 32 use favicon.svg, a small-size cut: Bold weight with the opening twice as wide, so the fold still reads as a line, not a smudge. It switches to light ink in dark mode through prefers-color-scheme.

Use

A few rules

Clear space

Leave space equal to the wordmark's x-height on every side of a lockup, and a quarter of the symbol's width around the symbol on its own.

Minimum size

Symbol 16 px with the favicon cut, 24 px otherwise. Horizontal lockup 96 px wide. Stacked lockup 64 px wide.

Colour

Ink on paper, light ink on night, or the accent symbol with an ink wordmark. Never set the e in two colours or fill the crease.

The crease

Always level, always the background showing through. Do not tilt it, extend it past the e, or animate it opening.

Wordmark

Outlined artwork only. Do not retype it in Literata: the spacing and the joined tt are drawn by hand.

Not allowed

Seals, quills, ribbons, envelope frames, outlines, shadows, gradients, or a second symbol beside the e.
