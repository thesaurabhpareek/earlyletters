# Code of conduct

Be kind and be plain. This applies to issues and any other space on this repository.

- Treat people with respect. Disagree with the idea, not the person.
- Keep to the point. Say what happened and what you expected.
- No harassment, insults, threats or discrimination.
- Do not share anyone's private information, and never post a child's name, photo or recording.
- Do not post spam or promotion.

## Reporting

If someone breaks these rules, email hello@earlyletters.com. For a security problem, use [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

## Enforcement

I own this repository and I decide. I may edit or delete a comment, close an issue, or block an account. I will read reports in good faith and keep them private. This is a small project, so replies may take a few days.
