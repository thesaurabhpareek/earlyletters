For counsel and the founder. Tracked as BL-527.

The closure review (`CLOSURE_REVIEW.md`) lists LNG-04, LNG-09, LNG-12, LNG-15 and LNG-18 as partly fixed. Every string that is still open is registered disclosure text. These strings are quoted word for word in `docs/legal/in-app-disclosures.md` or the consumer health data notice, and tests pin them. So these strings were not edited on this branch.

Each proposed rewrite follows the content rules: no em or en dashes, straight quotes, no ellipsis characters and no emoji. None implies that a machine writes the words, and none genders `{child}`.
