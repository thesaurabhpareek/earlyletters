Second-wave review: the first nine function reviews did not cover this area. Status: draft for Karma to rule. It changes no card, no code, no legal text and no model.

I am a Claude role, not a lawyer, not a licensed professional and not a regulator. Every legal reading below is marked "counsel must confirm". Where a requirement would add a new data flow, weaken a gate, or change what a person is told, it is a question for the founder or counsel, not a ruling.

The question this review asks of every card, in people's terms: a parent whispers a letter to Asha at 2 AM with a white-noise machine running. Do the right words appear, spelled the way the family spells them, with nothing added and nothing meaning-changing taken away? If the machine is unsure, does it say so, or keep quiet and guess? And does the original voice stay exactly as it was?

### 1.5 What is missing for a launch-ready, customer-first product

1. No card owns transcription accuracy. Spoken words are the product. NS-17 covers a second language, NS-39 the download, NS-34 speed, but no NS row states the accuracy a parent can expect in English, no row states what happens when the machine is unsure, and the frame's KPI table has no accuracy line.
2. Zero measured gates. No `F05-gate-*` evidence exists. The F05 bars (English 10% WER, Hindi 15%, Arabic 20% per dialect group, 95% names, zero phantom words) are proposals marked [R], never run on a phone (section 1.3).
3. The most common bedtime conditions are not gating. F05 7.1 reports whispered clips and speech over white noise "as separate rows and not gating at v1.0". The voice detector can drop a whisper entirely and then the letter has no words. For the evening parent this is the core loop.
4. Children and second speakers are unspecified. Whisper-family models do worse on child speech and two voices; we have no measurement and no card says what the parent sees. NS-16 forbids inviting a child to speak (R5) but does not say what happens when a toddler babbles in the recording.
5. Low confidence is not surfaced. `Flag` exists in core; card `09-capture.md:786` defers "a low-confidence Review line from `stt_meta`" to a "separate CAIO proposal". This is that proposal (FR-AIS-31 to 33). Today the parent cannot tell a doubtful word from a sure one.
6. The Hindi model is not hosted (`catalog.ts:179, 203` `hosted: false`), so a Hindi family today gets turbo, which ADR 0015 calls the weakest of our seven languages for Hindi.
7. Model files for six languages come from `huggingface.co` at a pinned revision, not from our host (`catalog.ts:35-60, 119-165`). The legal docs name it (`DATA_CLASSIFICATION.md:768`), but there is no requirement for a mirror if the file disappears or the host changes terms.
8. No licence record per model exists as a gate. FR-LEG-13 states one for packs and fonts; counsel packet Q9 (training-data terms) is live. No row says which of the five catalog models has a licence record, the training data read, and the date.
9. No model-change gate beyond the F05 staleness rule. F05-REQ-006 marks gates stale on any change but is "not built"; nothing stops a catalog edit from shipping.
10. `speech-config-lint.test.ts` (no `'auto'`, `translate` false, `tdrzEnable` off, F05-REQ-008, -009) and `language-gate.test.ts` do not exist in `apps/mobile/test/`. The behaviour is in `transcribe-run.ts:45`; no test fails if someone changes it.
11. AI law has no owner. `rg "AI Act" docs/legal docs/adr` found only a generic line in `compliance-register.md:3`. No requirement says whether Article 50 or a biometric law applies.
12. Agent safety is specified for the harness in general (`OPERATING_MODEL.md:11`: only the founder's instructions count) but not for the two cases that touch this area: a spoken transcript or a model reply that contains instructions, and harness agents reading content files.
13. Nothing states a refusal script for feature requests that would rewrite words ("make it nicer", summarise, translate, a tidy toggle, a "suggest a first" feature, Apple Writing Tools). The cards refuse each separately (06-firsts line 57, NS-17-R3, NS-01-R50); no one place lists the pattern.
14. Battery, memory and heat budgets exist (F05 section 9: 2% battery per 2-minute letter, 1.4 GB peak, 0 jetsam in 30 runs, p90 30 s) but no NS row carries them; NS-34 speed covers launch and navigation, not transcription (I read its rows R1 to R7).
15. The edit-pass LLM is not in the product (ADR 0003: rules first, hosted model behind a flag). I found no app code that builds a model provider (grep for `modelChangeRatio` and `source: 'model'` under `apps/mobile/src` returned nothing; the rest of `clean.ts` not read). A future model pass needs a gate that exists before the first line is written.
