# Parked and not built: what we said no to, and what would change our mind

Status: Draft v1, 19 Nov 2026. Author: Karma with the Chief Product Officer role. Scope is mostly saying no. Each line names who asked, why we wait or refuse, and the evidence that would reopen it. Designers do not draw these.

| Candidate | Rank | Who it would serve | Decision | What would change it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| C-09 Hands-free start | 20 | P1 with both hands full | A time-boxed spike only (NS-04). No doorway ships from it | The spike's exit criteria pass on a phone and App Review rules allow it |
| C-10 Quick capture from a doorway | 32 | P1 who wants one tap from the lock screen | After the spike; opens only when Ready on that phone (ruling 6, 19 Nov) | NS-04 exit passes; a reliability record of zero unmeant recordings in testing |
| C-32 Pictures follow the account | 37 | P1 on a new phone | Not built: no picture leaves the phone in the next builds (ruling 3, 19 Nov) | A security read and a counsel read, then a founder decision. Until then the Photo words say where a picture lives |
| C-30 A past letter on the Home Screen | 45 | P1 who likes widgets | Waits: a letter's words on the Home Screen are visible to anyone who picks up the phone | Evidence parents want it, and a design that shows no words by default |
| C-34 New first-run story cards | 46 | A first-time parent | Held until the account door is fixed and counsel has read the lines; the cards already built stand (ruling 7) | Counsel's read; tester notes that people do not understand what the app does |
| C-39 Android | 47 | P2 with an Android phone | Not now: a second platform doubles every test | Recruiting shows a large share of mixed-platform couples (study U8) |
| C-40 Native tab bar look | 48 | Everyone, slightly | Not now: risk to accessibility for a visual change | Apple's own control meets the accessibility floor in a device walk |
| C-38 Apple Watch | 49 | P1 with a watch | Not now: a second native target and a reliability risk | The spike shows wrist capture is safe and parents ask for it |
| C-36 Weekly family recap | 50 | Grandparents | Refused: a machine-made summary breaks the constitution | Never in that form. A parent-chosen share of one letter (NS-05) is the answer |
| C-42 Milestone notes ("100 letters") | 51 | Nobody | Refused: counts are banned | Never |
| C-41 Paper sounds | 52 | Nobody who has a sleeping baby | Refused for now: sound can wake a baby | A design that is silent by default and evidence anyone wants it |
| C-35 Gifts and a lifetime plan | 53 | A grandparent buying it | Not now: payments work, and "lifetime" is a promise in the founder's name | A founder pricing decision with the CBO, CFO and CLO roles; counsel |
| C-37 A Share tab | 54 | Nobody yet | Refused: Share is one verb on one letter and fails the founder's tab test | Share one letter proves daily use and passes the tab checklist in NS-05 section 10 |
| C-22 Co-parent "coming soon" | dropped | P2, waiting | Dropped on the founder's answer of 19 Nov: nothing says "coming soon" in any build | Co-parent ships for real (NS-25) |
