## NS-51. Photo moments (level 3) (candidate C-xx; kind Attractive; area A Capture; extends NS-49; drop C, spike first)

### 1. Who and when
A parent who took pictures of {child} this week and has not found the time to say what they were about. Job: "My phone shows me the days I took pictures, and I decide whether to say something about one."

### 2. Why
The founder asked for Apple Journal's flow with library access (`26` header): see what happened, then pick pictures and say the letter. Level 2 (NS-49) shows only what Apple's system lists. This card lets the app list days of pictures on the phone itself. Evidence: the founder's direction; Apple's and Google's products both lead with such moments and both give the person an off switch and a notification switch (S1, S4); no data on our parents. The cost is the trust line "never read a picture the parent did not pick", which this card narrows to "reads only dates, only after opt-in, only on the phone". The words stay the parent's.

### 3. What, and Not in scope
**What.**
1. **Opt-in, once, from Me, then the system sheet.** One Settings row "Photo moments" (words by `content`; Me, Reminders area, with the NS-46 Days row as neighbour, no new tab). The parent reads one plain sentence and taps; only then does iOS ask (S2: ask on a direct action, never at launch). The parent answers in Apple's sheet: limited or full.
2. **Moments.** From picture dates only (NS-51-R5), the phone finds a day or a few hours with several pictures, or a day matching a birthday, a month age, or a day the parent marked (NS-46, read in memory).
3. **Where it shows.** In the capture cluster only: the "Start from a photo" menu (NS-49-R29) gains a third item when a moment exists, which opens the moment sheet (A-S17, PROPOSED) of the parent's own pictures. The parent picks one or several; capture opens with them (NS-52). No Home tile, no slip, no strip (R-IA-3), no count.
4. **Nudge.** One of the existing budgeted reminders (NS-32), neutral words, only when the app was opened since the pictures were taken (no background work, Q-PM-8).
5. **Controls.** Albums, pause, off with index removal, a way to change the limited selection.
**Not in scope.** Faces, people, scenes, objects, places, album names shown outside the choice sheet, any caption or title, a count of moments or of unwritten days, a comparison, a background fetch or observer, video analysis, any upload, a Home tile, Android, any nudge text that describes a picture, "memories" or "best".

### 4. Happy path
1. Me, "Photo moments": one sentence; the parent taps "Turn on". Apple's sheet appears; the parent chooses "Select Photos" or allows all.
2. The phone reads picture dates (not pixels) and keeps a small index on the phone.
3. Next time the parent opens Home, "Start from a photo" opens a menu with a third item. They open it, see their own pictures from one weekend, choose two.
4. Capture opens with those two pictures and an empty photo-words field. They speak. Review, Keep. The letter holds two stored copies and no mark of where they came from.

### 5. Unhappy paths and edge cases

| Case | What happens |
|---|---|
| Permission denied or restricted | The row says calmly it is off in Settings and offers "Open Settings" when the parent opens the row; no other screen of ours mentions it; no re-ask, no nag (NS-51-R19) |
| Limited access | Works on the selection; no moment if the selection is small; "Choose which pictures" in the row opens Apple's limited-library picker; the automatic prompt is suppressed (NS-51-R18) |
| Limited access and albums | The album choice is hidden (S2: no user albums); the selection is the scope |
| iCloud original not downloaded | The index needs only dates, so nothing downloads. A thumbnail that is not on the phone shows a plain box; choosing it follows NS-52-R9 (NS-51-R21) |
| No space | Index is tiny; a full phone never blocks the row or the sheet (NS-51-R38); attach follows NS-52-R10 |
| Deleted source | A picture deleted after indexing drops from the sheet quietly; a moment below threshold vanishes; no message (NS-51-R22) |
| Restored phone, new phone | The index and the opt-in are not in the backup; the feature is off and the ask does not return by itself (NS-51-R23) |
| Second account on the phone | The opt-in and index are per account; account B starts off; B never sees A's index (NS-51-R24) |
| App lock | The sheet needs unlock; the nudge is neutral; the switcher shows no thumbnail (NS-51-R25) |
| Library empty, or no moment | Nothing shows; no line says so |
| Parent changes the limited selection in Settings | The index is rebuilt on the next foreground; nothing is said |
| Flag off or paused remotely | Row and menu item absent; Erase, sweep and Export still work (NS-51-R32) |
| iPad, Mac app, Expo Go, Android | Absent, not greyed (NS-49-R18 pattern) |

### 6. Scenario coverage

| Scenario | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| First use | Off until the parent turns it on; no mention elsewhere |
| Empty, normal, many | Empty: nothing. Many: at most the newest moment is offered (NS-51-R8); no list of past moments |

| Signed out | The index is cleared at sign-out (NS-51-R17); the book is kept (D1) |
| New phone | As NS-51-R23 |

## NS-52. Pictures and videos with a letter (candidate C-xx; kind Attractive; area A Capture, B Book; extends NS-01; drop C, spike first)

### 1. Who and when
A parent who wants the letter about a day to keep the day: two pictures from the park and ten seconds of {child} laughing. Job: "I keep what I said and what it looked like together."

### 2. Why
The founder: pictures matter most, and videos too (`26` section 1). Remento accepts a photo and then voice or video (S6, REPO), Apple's Journal describes adding "photos, drawings, videos, audio" (S3, description only). One picture was a launch simplification (NS-01), not a rule of the product. Evidence on parents' demand: none read. Videos are the heaviest data we could hold (`functions/feature-opportunities.md:107`), which is why limits, storage, label and counsel gates are rows here.

### 3. What, and Not in scope
**What.**
1. **Several pictures and videos per letter.** Defaults (Q-PM-2): 6 pictures and 2 videos, video up to 60 seconds, stored video up to 100 MB. A letter still needs words (D-103); media alone is not a letter.
2. **Two paths in.** The system picker with multi-select (S2: supports photos and videos) from "Start from a photo" and from Review's Add control; and the NS-51 moment sheet. Level 2 still adds one photo (NS-49).
3. **Storage.** Stored copies on the phone: pictures as NS-01 (stripped, 4,032 px, JPEG 0.85); videos re-encoded to a capped size with metadata removed.
4. **Reading.** The letter page shows pictures in order; a video shows a poster frame and plays on tap.
5. **Keeping.** Save a copy and Export include the media; the parent can remove one item or the whole letter, and every file goes with it.
6. **Sync (Q-PM-3).** Variant A (default): media stay on the phone and in the iPhone backup. Variant B: media sync encrypted to the family's book. Both are specified below; B is built only on the founder's answer.
7. **Print.** Pictures print; video does not.
**Not in scope.** Camera capture, trimming, editing, filters, music, a slideshow or "story" made from the media, captions or alt text written by the machine, any description or classification of media, transcription of video sound (Q-PM-4), Live Photo motion, sharing a video through NS-05, a QR to a video page (Q-PM-10), a feed of media, Android.

### 4. Happy path
1. Home, "Start from a photo", "Choose pictures and videos". Apple's picker opens in its own process; the parent picks three pictures and one video.
2. Ready appears with the four items and an empty photo-words field per item. The video is copied, checked and compressed with a progress line; Cancel is always there.
3. The parent speaks; Review shows the items in order, with Remove on each; Keep.
4. In the Book, the letter shows pictures and a poster frame; tapping plays the video with its own sound only after a tap.

### 5. Unhappy paths and edge cases

| Case | What happens |

| Photos permission | None is needed for the picker. Denied or limited library access (NS-51) never affects the picker path |

### 8. Non-functional budgets
- Latency: attach one picture under NS-01's budget; a 60 s 1080p video re-encodes in under 60 s on an SE 3 (ASSUMED; the spike measures), with progress throughout.
- Memory: stream, never hold a whole video in memory.
- On-device storage: a letter at the limits is about 6 pictures at 2 to 4 MB each plus 2 videos at up to 100 MB (ASSUMED); the iPhone backup grows by the same; Me, Storage shows it.
- Battery: a transcode is foreground only.
- Offline: phone files work; iCloud-only items wait.
- Accessibility: NS-52-R28. Localisation: strings in packs. Privacy: NS-52-R44.
- Variant B transfer: cost and cap per family are `finance`'s open item.

### 12. Door lines
Adding a video of a child is an act, and under Variant B a one-way door.
- **Shown:** Apple's picker; the limits line; under Variant B one sentence before the first upload (intent only, counsel reads): where the files go, who sees them (the book's members), that they can be deleted.
- **Records:** Variant A: the files on the phone. Variant B: the files on our servers and an upload record.
- **Never:** reading the contents of media; transcribing sound; a caption or description by the machine; sharing a video through our share paths; a count; a background upload without Variant B consent; asking again after a no.
- **Decline:** Cancel in the picker; remove any item; under Variant B, "Keep on this phone only" per letter (a founder decision with Q-PM-3).
