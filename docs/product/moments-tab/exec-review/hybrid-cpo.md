## Verdict: ACCEPT-WITH-CHANGES

The hybrid is the right shape and I would not go back to either alternative. It is not yet the smallest release that keeps the quality bar, and two things in the delta read stronger than the evidence under them.

Counts: 0 BLOCKER, 8 MAJOR, 5 MINOR (13 findings).

Conditions (each is a finding below):
1. One door, not two, on a parent's first look at the tab (EXH-CPO-1, EXH-CPO-2).
2. Present Apple's picker as a sheet first; hold the inline host until a device read says it is worth its cost (EXH-CPO-3, EXH-CPO-8).
3. Keep one plain enlarge in the day sheet; defer only pinch, pan and swipe (EXH-CPO-4).
4. Say plainly what the library read does for return visits in the first release, and stop calling the store story stronger until the CCO reads the rule (EXH-CPO-5, EXH-CPO-7).
5. Give the founder a way to read which door parents use (EXH-CPO-6).

## The three tests you asked for

### Does the hybrid beat library-read-only and picker-only?

| Test | Library-read-only (the spec as frozen) | Picker-only (the earlier delta draft) | Hybrid | My read |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| First-time experience | A parent who says no meets calm cards but has no action on the tab beyond Home's own pictures route; the spec's own flag-off row sends them to "Add pictures" through the existing system picker (spec 8.1) | A real action with no ask, but nothing says "here is a day that is yours"; the first screen is a button | Real cards first, an ask, and a working door after a no | Hybrid wins, by a modest margin. The gain is the no path, not the yes path [A] |
| Return visits | Return value comes from For you cards, dated cards and the question | Almost none: a button is a pull, not a reason to return | Same as library-read-only; the picker adds nothing to return | Hybrid equals library-read-only, and both beat picker-only clearly. The picker is not a return mechanism and the delta says so (section 9) [F, delta 9] |
| Trust | Rests on the primer and Apple's sheet | Highest: Apple's own picker, only what the parent chose | A trust ladder is possible: pick first, allow later | Hybrid wins on trust, but only if the two doors do not collide (EXH-CPO-1, EXH-CPO-2) [A] |
| Failure case (a gate fails) | Tab ships on dated cards and prompts | Unaffected | Tab ships with dated cards plus a real action; the door is under F1 only (delta Q5) | Hybrid wins clearly; this is its strongest argument [F, delta 6, 9] |
| Cost | Spec 19.4: 26 PRs, about 7,600 changed lines (ASSUMED in the spec) | Not sized in anything I read | Delta says roughly neutral and treats it as zero, with every size ASSUMED (delta 6) | I accept "neutral". The real shift is from lines to founder minutes (EXH-CPO-8) [E] |

Net: hybrid beats both on the failure case and on trust, ties library-read-only on return, and its first-time gain is the declined path. That is a real but not large gain, which is the right size for a delta.

### Is the tab worth a place?

Yes, conditionally, and the condition is not the hybrid. The founder ruled the tab and the library read, and I do not reopen them. My own view, stated plainly: the daily-return claim has no parent evidence (spec 4.4 and section 20 call it a HYPOTHESIS; NS-53-R260 bars saying otherwise). What the tab earns today is a place for starting a letter from something already in hand (job 5 in the spec 1.3) and a no-permission floor. What it has not earned is a daily visit.

Two facts the delta does not weigh:
- On a day with no new pictures and no marked day, the tab's one honest card is a question from the 111 prompts (spec 4.4), and Home already owns "Today's prompt" (spec 4.3 table). The one-owner rule (NS-53-R5) governs duplicate facts. If the prompt shown on Moments is the same prompt as Home's, a parent with no library access has nothing new on the tab on most days [A, my reading of 4.3 and 4.4]. Library access, not the picker, is what makes the tab different from Home.
- The picker door duplicates Home's "Start from a photo" and capture's Add (spec 4.3; delta NEW-2). The delta concedes the door is "mostly discoverability" (delta 9). I agree, and I would not count it toward the tab's place.

The tab keeps its place on the existing stop line: if trial-to-paid or first renewal after the tab is worse than before for two consecutive install weeks by the founder's margin, F1 pauses (spec 20.1; the margin is O-46, still the founder's). That stop line, not the hybrid, is what makes the bet safe.

### What is the smallest set that keeps the quality bar?

Keep:
- The library read, For you, Recent, day sheet, place and date lines, dated and occasion cards, capture hand-off (the rulings).
- One `AttachRequest`, one stripper, three producers (delta R88 change).
- Apple's picker as a presented sheet from three places: the tab door in the states where the library is not in use, the day sheet's "Choose other pictures" under Limited access, and capture Add (delta NEW-2, with EXH-CPO-1 narrowing the first).
- The date chip only, offered and never preselected (delta Q3, R95).
- NEW-1 (a picked picture is never grouped, ranked, labelled or given a place), NEW-5, NEW-7, and NEW-8 (device answers before the native merge).
- Drop R56 and R57 (delta Q2).
- One plain enlarge in the day sheet (EXH-CPO-4).
- PR 18b early, as the delta says: it needs no library gate and makes the fallback real.

Defer or cut:
- The inline host and its sheet chrome (NEW-4, part of NEW-3) until a device read says a presented picker is not enough (EXH-CPO-3).
- The picker door on the tab body in the never-asked state (EXH-CPO-1).
- Pinch, pan and swipe in the viewer (already on the cut list).
- G1a unknowns (e) and (f) until the inline host is back on the table (EXH-CPO-8).
