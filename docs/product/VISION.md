# Early Letters: product vision (19 Nov 2026)

Owner: the Chief Product Officer role, with Karma. Status: Draft for the founder. One page that every requirement, design and build decision is checked against.

Nothing here is new. Every line restates a decision or a requirement already on record, with its source. Where the record is thin, this page says so. If a line here and its source disagree, the source wins and this page is wrong.

## Who it is for

A tired parent with a few free minutes: after the baby is down, or during a feed with one hand free, often in the dark (persona P1, `docs/prd/v2/02-customers.md` section 3). Then the other parent (P2), the expecting parent (P3), the family that speaks more than one language (P4), close family later (P5), and the child who reads it years from now (P6).

It is not for a parent who wants the app to write for them, for anyone under 18, for a public or social feed, or for a photo album first (same file, "Not for").

## The promise

What we say in public, the founder's decision of 1 Nov 2026 (D-139): letters to your child, in your own words and voice.

The internal promise behind it (`03-goals-and-principles.md` section 1; BRAND.md and 03 still lead with the older descriptor and should be updated to match D-139): Early Letters is the baby memory book you fill by talking. The words stay yours: every small fix is shown and can be undone, your recording is kept as you made it, and each letter is filed by your child's age for your child to read and hear for years (`docs/prd/v2/03-goals-and-principles.md` section 1).

Four things must stay true: your words with every fix in view; kept; private; yours always. While recording backup is off, "kept" means kept on this phone and in any copy the parent saves; nothing we say may suggest more.

## The rule above every feature

The machine may remove and repair. It may never add meaning (`CLAUDE.md`). No feature writes, rewrites, summarises, translates or shapes a person's words.

## What a parent must never meet

A lost letter or recording. A word they did not say. A charge they did not expect, or a past letter locked behind payment. A count of what they missed, a streak or a guilt line. Their child's name or words on a lock screen without their choice. A notification about a due date. Their language translated or changed without asking (`02-customers.md` section 6).

## The jobs, in order

1. Never lose what I made.
2. Let me capture it in the few minutes I have.
3. Leave my words and voice for my child's future.
4. Keep my child's memories private.
5. Tell me what to write about, without nagging.
6. Let family see the baby grow.
7. Give me a finished book I can hold.
8. Keep our languages in the family.
9. Let me give it as a gift.

(`02-customers.md` section 4. Importance is a judgment; evidence is from other products' reviews and public sources. We have no evidence from our own families yet.)

## The shape of the app

Three tabs, one verb each: Home is where you say it, Book is where you read it, Me is where you look after it. Only Home starts a letter. Family is never a tab; the Library is not a tab; Share is not a tab (closed questions N1 to N3, 11 Nov; D-161 as drafted in #827; the founder's answer of 19 Nov).

## The next six months, in one line each

- **Now (Build 12, the first store release):** sign-in, sync, free plan and Plus, English, recording backup off (D-157 to D-160).
- **Next (Builds 13 and 14):** make what exists safer and truer, then a photo with a letter, finding a letter by a word, and sharing one letter as text (closed question X1; exec rulings of 19 Nov).
- **Then:** the people around the child (hearing a letter, co-parent in store builds), the physical keepsake (a print-ready file first), and a longer life for the book (firsts, sealed letters). That order is the founder's working order from 10 Nov (`exec-rulings-2026-11-10-expansion.md`, Q4).
- **Always out:** trackers, health or feeding logs, recaps written by a machine, moods, streaks, counts, seen-by receipts, translation (same record).

## How we will know

Two headline numbers are on record: paying families over first-time downloads by install week, and weekly making families (`03-goals-and-principles.md` section 4.1). Analytics is off through the 1.0 line (D-124), so today neither can be read from the app. Until that changes, we learn from App Store Connect, from testers, and from numbers testers send by hand (the app composes and sends nothing; see the product frame, section 3). Say this plainly whenever a target is quoted.

## What this page does not settle

Dates and what is in each release (the founder's). Whether a picture or a voice ever leaves the phone beyond the owner's backup (closed question X2: not before a security and counsel read). Print as a business (no spend, no partner, no seller role before an entity). The legal reading of anything about children's data: counsel must confirm.
