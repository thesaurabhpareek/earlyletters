# Recruiting note: 8 to 10 parents (NS-03)

Status: DRAFT, 24 Nov 2026, `user-research` (Claude role). Counsel reads this note before it is sent (NS-03 section 0). Not sent to anyone yet.

Source: NS-03 sections 1, 5 and 7 (`docs/product/prd/03-family-hears-it-pilot.md`). The card's recruit text is the intent; the wording below is proposed, with changes listed in the PR body.

## 1. Who to ask

From the founder's invited list of TestFlight and alpha families only.

- Ask: adults 18 or over, each with a child under 5 (or on the way), with at least 3 letters kept in {brand.name}.
- Aim: 8 to 10 who say yes. If the list allows, at least 2 whose chosen person lives in another country (only once counsel's Q22 answer covers them), and at least 2 with a letter in a language other than English. The current store build writes down English only (D-121), so that second group may be 0.
- Don't ask: anyone under 18, anyone the founder pays, an App Store reviewer, anyone who works for a competitor, or anyone in acute crisis right now.
- Two parents of the same child may each take part, separately, with their own code. Their answers are never joined.

## 2. How to send it

- One parent per message, from the founder's own phone, in the thread the founder already has with them. Never in a group chat, so nobody learns who else is taking part.
- Only after counsel's read is on record in the pilot file (NS-03-R1).
- The window is 5 working days. If fewer than 8 have said yes by the end, extend once by 3 working days. If still fewer than 8, stop and re-plan (stop rule 5).
- No contact on a US federal public holiday.
- Track only numbers in the counts file: how many were asked, how many said yes. Names stay in the founder's own messages and the private key.

## 3. The note

> Hi. We're trying out a small idea and would value your help.
> If you're up for it, you'd send one of your letters from {brand.name} to a grandparent or someone else close to you, in your own message, and answer a few short questions about how it went over the next week. We'd never receive it.
> It takes about 15 minutes in all, spread over a week. It's fine to say no.
> Would you like to hear more?

## 4. If they'd like to hear more: four quick checks

> Thanks. Four quick checks first:
> 1. Are you 18 or older?
> 2. Is your child under 5, or on the way?
> 3. Do you have at least 3 letters kept in {brand.name}?
> 4. Is there one person, family or a close friend, you'd be happy to send a letter to?
> If it's yes to all four, we'll send a short note on what taking part means, and you can decide then.

Yes to all four: send the consent text for their arm (`02-consent-texts-DRAFT-FOR-COUNSEL.md`, and the arm rule in `01-session-script.md` section 3).

## 5. Replies

**Not a fit this time (any check is no):**

> Thanks for checking. This round needs all four, so it isn't a fit this time. Nothing changes in the app.

**No, or no reply after one gentle follow-up:**

> No problem at all, and thanks for reading.

Then no further contact about the pilot.
