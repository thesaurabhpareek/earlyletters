# Child safety reports: a brief for a licensed lawyer

Status: DRAFT. This is not a procedure. It is the brief for a licensed US lawyer that CLO-46 (`docs/agents/exec/RULINGS.md`) recommends engaging before launch, and the interim rule that holds until legal advice is recorded. Written by Claude roles, not counsel. Under D-172 there is no outside lawyer; the CLO flags that the exec-team read is not enough here because these questions touch US federal criminal law. Engaging a lawyer is the founder's decision (a payment and a change to D-172). If he declines, the exec team answers these questions under D-172 and the procedure says that no lawyer read it.

## 1. The interim rule, until legal advice is recorded

- Nobody opens, previews, downloads, forwards, copies, quotes, moves or deletes the material, an attachment or a link. The message stays where it arrived.
- Only the founder can reach that mailbox. No Claude session, connector or script may read it. Which mail service receives `hello@` is UNVERIFIED (claims row EF1).
- The only reply is the acknowledgement, macro `policy.abuseMaterialReport` in `docs/support/macros.md` (FR-POL-02). If a child is in danger now, the safety pointer goes first (FR-SUP-16).
- The founder takes the kind and the date, never the email or a link, to the exec team the same day.
- Nobody files a report with NCMEC until legal advice is recorded (question 8 asks whether that wait fits the statute). A Claude role never files.
- The message is not deleted when its ticket closes (FR-BKO-35, CLO-46).
- Nothing is promised to the person who reported: no outcome, no timing, no follow-up.

## 2. Questions for the lawyer

1. Are we a "provider" under 18 U.S.C. 2258E(6), for mail sent to the support address, and for letters stored in a book that two parents share?
2. Does a report email that describes or attaches suspected material give us "actual knowledge" under 2258A(a)(1) if nobody opens it?
3. If we report, what must we preserve under 2258A(h) (one year, per Pub. L. 118-59), where, and with whose access? How does that fit leaving the message unopened, our retention promises and a person's deletion rights?
4. May the founder hold the message unopened in our mail provider's mailbox? Does the provider's own scanning change anything?
5. Is the acknowledgement wording acceptable, and should it point the reporter to NCMEC's CyberTipline?
6. Is Terms 9 ("We report apparent child sexual abuse material to the National Center for Missing and Exploited Children as US law requires") accurate for us?
7. What does the founder do with a message that turns out to be wrongly flagged?
8. 2258A(a)(1) asks for a report "as soon as reasonably possible after obtaining actual knowledge". Does the interim rule, that nobody files until you have advised, fit that, and what should the founder do with a report that arrives before you are engaged?
