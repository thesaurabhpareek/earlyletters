# 09a. Review and merge practice, rules from 21 to 24 Nov

Companion to chapter 09 (owner `ai-eng-lead`), kept apart so chapter 09 stays inside 220 lines. Rules AIE-R40 to R43 (21 Nov) and AIE-R44 to R50 (22 to 24 Nov) follow AIE-R32 to R39 in chapter 09. In force on merge under CR-73. What enforces each rule, and what does not yet, is in `ENFORCEMENT.md`.

**AIE-R40 (MUST)** A PR is not merged on required checks that ran on a base behind `develop` by commits touching a workspace the PR also touches: update the branch, wait for `required` again, and get fresh counted reviews (the update is a new commit). *Why:* a green run on an old base says nothing about the merged result. *Evidence:* #1128 had green `required` from 05:26 UTC (and `tests and content rules` from 05:23) on a base that was about 14 hours old when it merged at 18:42 UTC; it met #839 (merged about 16:46 UTC) and develop was red until #1353. Branch protection has `strict: false` (read 21 Nov); strict would force this but makes every merge re-run CI behind a queue that held 86 to 150 queued runs on 20 and 21 Nov (counted by REST then, not re-checkable now). *Enforced by:* `merge-queue.mjs` NEEDS-UPDATE verdict, merged in #1356 (AIE-G16). It is a verdict the queue prints, not a blocking check: a merger can still ignore it, so review still applies.

**AIE-R41 (MUST)** When `develop` is red, one session owns the fix: before starting, search open and just-merged PRs for the failing test names, and post one comment on the PR that broke `develop` naming the fix PR. *Why:* two sessions fixed the same break. *Evidence:* #1353 (merged 19:19 UTC, two counted reviews) and #1354 (closed as a duplicate after two review rounds) fixed the same failing tests, which #1353's title attributes to #1128 and #837 (I did not re-check the count of failures or which of the two caused each). *Enforced by:* review only (AIE-G17).

**AIE-R42 (MUST)** Reviewers who mutate files work in their own scratch worktree, never in the shared checkout and never two at once in the same one. *Why:* a mutation left in a shared tree reads as a defect in the PR. *Evidence:* on #1354 two reviewers edited `erase-picture.ts` in one checkout at the same time and one reported lines it had not added (the reviewer's hand-back in the session record; it is not in the PR comments); the pushed head was clean. *Enforced by:* the reviewer brief; review only.

**AIE-R43 (MUST)** A merge message states the real basis of approval: CR-70 and CR-73 for a PR Karma approved, or a quoted founder chat line for one that needs him. *Why:* a fixed template line said "founder approved" on merges he did not approve. *Evidence:* 7 merges after CR-73 (ae0c4e578, b0e422443, ace71fa05, 1e19e6439, 7ada1b30d, ee782b8f2, 9018cdf2a) carry the founder line; history is not rewritten and the record is `infra/develop-checks-and-corrections-2026-11-21.md` in the Project. *Enforced by:* not yet (AIE-G18).

**AIE-R44 (MUST)** Before a pinned merge, a merger whose PR touches `packages/analytics`, `packages/content` strings or `docs/content/map/*` runs those workspaces' tests on the merged tree (a scratch merge with current `develop`), not on the PR head. *Why:* the review and the green run were both taken before `develop` moved. *Evidence:* 23 Nov, two merges broke `develop` for every open PR: #1509 and #1510 merged after #1688 had made a stale content map fail `npm test`, and #1259 (analytics send path reads the registry) merged on a base older than #1533 and the NS-54 phone test, so four analytics tests failed; the fix went in as #1740 (the record is `infra/karma-queue-drain-claims-2026-11-23.md` in the Project). *Enforced by:* review only (AIE-G19). AIE-R40 covers the same hazard through the `merge-queue.mjs` verdict, which only helps when it is read before the merge.

**AIE-R45 (MUST)** When `required` is red on a PR because `develop` was red, and `develop` is fixed, close and reopen the PR to get a fresh run; do not rerun failed jobs. *Why:* a rerun of failed jobs reuses the merge ref made when the run started, so it tests the old base. The head and the counted reviews stay valid, because the head does not change. If a merge is refused with "Required status check compliance-fence is cancelled" while the required checks are green, remove and re-add one of the PR's labels (`agent:<handle>` or `karma:approved`, whichever the PR carries) to start a fresh run; the fence re-reads the approvals at the head on every run, so the approvals still have to name the current head (the record is `infra/karma-work-ledger-2026-11-24-session-017c.md` in the Project). *Evidence:* #454 and #1719 turned `required` green after a close and reopen on 23 Nov; #1731 did the same. A close and reopen that stays red (#1711) means a real failure: read the job log before reopening again. *Enforced by:* review only.

**AIE-R46 (MUST)** A review of a PR that changes an import, a module boundary or a pinned source string (a test that slices a file's text) runs the whole test suite of the touched app, or reads the result of `tests and content rules` at the head, before it says PASS. A focused run of the new tests is not enough. *Why:* the tests that break are the old ones that pin what the PR moved. *Evidence:* #1711 had two PASS reviews at 2e6600a1 from focused runs, then failed CI on `test/boundaries.test.ts` (a stale `review.tsx::expo-audio` entry in the known-breaks list) and `test/ns09-let-go-source.test.ts` (it pinned `const player = useAudioPlayer(`); a two-file test fix at 32552536 and fresh reviews were needed. *Enforced by:* the review-gate skill, section 4 (review only; AIE-G20).

**AIE-R47 (MUST)** PRs that append to a hot file are merged one at a time, each right after its reviews, and a conflict is resolved by keeping `develop`'s rows first and the PR's own after them, never dropping a row that is not yours. Hot files: `docs/agents/CLAIMS.md`, `docs/agents/exec/RULINGS.md`, `docs/agents/cto/RULINGS.md`, `docs/BACKLOG.md` and the generated `docs/content/map/*` (regenerate it with `node scripts/content/export-content-map.mjs` after taking `develop`'s copy; `scripts/content/merge-content-map.mjs` from #1813 is the helper). *Why:* every merge of one of these voids the reviews of the next, and a fixer that rewrote the file dropped other sessions' rows. *Evidence:* #1719 and #1267 re-conflicted after #454; #1712 twice on `CLAIMS.md`; #1732 once on `CLAIMS.md` and the old stacked commits, then twice more on the content map alone (23 Nov). *Enforced by:* `scripts/agents/append-only-check.mjs` flags edited or deleted lines, but only in the rulings and release records listed in `docs/agents/append-only.json`; it does not list `CLAIMS.md`, `BACKLOG.md` or the content map, and the order of merging is review only (AIE-G21).

**AIE-R48 (MUST)** A reviewer or fixer works in its own detached worktree under a unique name (for example with the PR number and a process id), with its own `node_modules` whose `@scribe/*` entries point at that worktree's `packages/`, and removes it afterwards. *Why:* a symlinked shared `node_modules` resolves `@scribe/core` and `@scribe/content` to an older checkout, so a run can pass or fail for the wrong reason, and a fixed name such as `/tmp/rv1732` is deleted or reused by another session. *Evidence:* on 23 Nov both reviewers of #1731 found their first run had used the main checkout's packages and redid it; `/tmp/rv1732` was deleted by another session mid-review, and `/tmp/wt1733` and `/tmp/rv1711` already existed when a reviewer tried to create them. A worktree built this way has no npm workspaces, so `test/licences.test.ts` fails there; CI is the check for that file. *Enforced by:* the reviewer brief; review only. This extends AIE-R42.

**AIE-R49 (SHOULD)** After a push that only merges `develop` or regenerates a generated file, the re-review covers the delta from the last passed head: confirm the push is a merge, that the PR's own files are unchanged (`git diff <old head> <new head>` on those paths is empty), and that the generator leaves the tree clean. It still posts a new header at the new head. *Why:* the delta reviews of #1712 and #1732 took one to three minutes each, where the full reviews of the same PRs had taken two to ten. *Evidence:* #1712 at 7983f78a and #1732 at 339d25eb (23 Nov), by the agents' own run times; those reviews also listed what they had not re-run. *Enforced by:* the review-gate skill, section 5 (review only).

**AIE-R50 (SHOULD)** Before launching a reviewer or fixer on a PR, read its comments for a claim or counted reviews, check `docs/agents/CLAIMS.md` and the PR's labels, and post a one-line claim comment. *Why:* two Karma sessions worked the same PRs on 23 and 24 Nov (claims by session 0hu2 on #1711 and #1731; session 017cUpp claiming #1822). *Evidence:* the claim comments on those PRs. *Enforced by:* review only (AIE-G17 covers the same-failing-test case).
