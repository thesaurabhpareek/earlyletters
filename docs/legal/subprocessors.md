## Who helps us run Early Letters

Version 2.1.0. Effective [effective date].

These companies handle information for us. Each acts only on our instructions, under a written contract, and may not sell your information or use your letters or recordings to train models.

| Company | What it does for us | What it handles |
|---|---|---|
| Supabase | Database, sign-in and server functions, including the service that restores backed-up recordings | Your account, your book, your letters, your plan status and the sealed recovery keys |
| Cloudflare R2 | Stores encrypted recording backups and delivers speech packs | Encrypted recordings, which it cannot open; your IP address when the app downloads a speech pack |
| Resend | Sends our emails | Your email address and the emails we send |
| Vercel | Hosts earlyletters.com | Website visits |

Our data is stored in the United States.

## Other companies

- **Apple** provides Sign in with Apple, App Store purchases, iCloud Keychain, iPhone backups and notification delivery under its own terms.
- **Hugging Face** is a public library of speech models. The app downloads some model files directly from it, so it sees your IP address and the file requested.

The app has no analytics or crash reporting service.

## Changes

We update this page before a new company handles your information, and we tell you in the app at least 30 days before a new company receives letters or recordings. For a company's privacy contact, write to hello@earlyletters.com.
