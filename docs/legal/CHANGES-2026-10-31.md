### Terms of Service 4.0.0, section 14 note

Rewritten in 1.5.0 for founder decision D-053 and ADR 0013: Plus is sold only through the App Store, shown with Apple's own SubscriptionStoreView, and checked on the device (`Transaction.currentEntitlements`); no server of ours sees purchases (no App Store Server Notifications, no App Store Server API, no purchase ledger).
