## Still open for the founder and counsel (not fixed by these edits)

1. **California 17602(b) notices in the first store build (SUB-03).** COMPLIANCE 5.1 reads (b)(1) (free trial over 31 days: the annual plan's 2-month trial) and (b)(2) (initial term of a year or more) as requiring a notice with "the amount and frequency of charges", and (h) an annual reminder with the amount. In build 1 nothing from us states the amount: the app reminders, the in-app note and the confirmation name none (D-083), and no email is sent (D-121). Whether Apple's own messages reach these people inside the windows, and with the amount, is **Unverified**. COMPLIANCE 5.1 also marks part of (h) **partly Unverified** and leaves open whether the duties fall on us when Apple is merchant of record. Choose before submission: (i) a narrow D-083 exception so the long trial reminder, annual reminder, in-app note and confirmation show StoreKit's `displayPrice` and period (then Terms 14.6 changes again), or (ii) counsel's written view that Apple's messages are enough.
2. **Purchase acknowledgment without the amount (SUB-04).** 17602(a)(3); same choice as item 1.
3. **Free promise scope (Q14).** Co-parent writing is out of the permanent list. Also `packages/core/src/plan.ts` says a holder's Plus covers every member of a book where the holder is a parent, while Terms 14.12 says a co-parent outside the Apple family group needs their own Plus. One of them must change before co-parent sharing ships.
4. **Keychain free-letter count** is now disclosed in Terms 14.1 (D-083 asks counsel to confirm it is acceptable).
5. "Apple's receipt shows the amount you were charged" (Terms 14.6, Subscription Terms) is inferred from Apple's usual receipts, not seen for these products. Check it in the TestFlight sandbox pass.
