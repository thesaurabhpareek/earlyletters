## 5. Our transcription promise

5.1 We never rewrite your words. The app turns speech into text on your iPhone and may only remove filler sounds, false starts and accidental repeats; spell a name the way your names and words list does; add punctuation and paragraph breaks; and fix a one-word grammar slip.

5.2 It never adds, removes or changes meaning, reorders sentences, summarizes, translates or alters your recording. The original transcript is kept unchanged, and you can see and undo every fix.

5.3 This version writes down English only. Speech recognition can mishear, especially with noise, accents or more than one language. Read each letter before you keep it. Letters are a family keepsake, not a medical, legal or professional record.

5.4 The speech model is a large one-time download, from Hugging Face, which the app shows and asks about before it starts. Until it is ready, recordings are kept and the words follow.

6.2 We hold no copy of your book. If your iPhone is lost or broken, or you delete the app, we cannot bring your letters or recordings back. Please keep your own copy.
