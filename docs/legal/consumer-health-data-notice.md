# Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Version 2.0.0. Effective date: TBD.

Some US state laws, such as Washington's My Health My Data Act and Nevada's consumer health data law, give special protection to "consumer health data". This short policy says how Early Letters treats it. We follow it for everyone in the United States. Our Privacy Policy at https://earlyletters.com/privacy covers everything else.

## 1. Why a memory book may hold health details

Early Letters does not ask about anyone's health and does not look at what you write. But letters are free-form, so you may mention a fever, a hospital stay, a pregnancy or how you are feeling. If you start a book before your child is born, you can enter a due date. Under these laws that can count as consumer health data.

## 2. What we collect

**From the app: none.** Your letters, recordings, due date and child details stay on your phone. We do not receive them, and the app has no account or sync. The app does not connect to health apps or devices, use your location, or guess anything about anyone's health.

**From the website:** only an email address, if you ask us to tell you when the app is ready. That is not health information.

## 3. How we use and share it

We do not use or share consumer health data, because we do not receive it. We never sell it, use it for ads, or use it to train machine learning models. If you export a letter or share a recording, you decide who gets it.

## 4. Your rights

You can ask whether we hold consumer health data about you, get a copy and a list of who received it, ask us to delete it, and withdraw consent. Because we hold none from the app, there is usually nothing to give or delete. Write to hello@earlyletters.com and we will check and answer. If we say no to a request, reply with "Appeal" in the subject and we will look again, and tell you how to contact your state attorney general if we still decline.

## 5. Later versions

When sign-in, sync or writing with a co-parent arrives, letters could leave your phone. Before that, we will update this policy and ask for your separate, clear agreement before anything you wrote is sent to us.
