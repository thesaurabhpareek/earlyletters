# Early Letters Plus: Subscription Terms

Version 4.1.0. Effective [effective date].

These terms are part of the Early Letters Terms of Service (https://earlyletters.com/terms), section 8. Apple sells and bills Plus. Apple's subscription screen shows the exact price and dates before you subscribe, and those are the terms that apply to you.

## What is free

Your first 2 letters are free. Every letter you keep stays yours to read, play and export, with or without Plus. If you never subscribe, or stop later, every letter and recording you kept stays yours to read, play and export, and every book you already have stays readable.

Reading, playing recordings, exporting and deleting are free, always. Read together and starting a book for another child do not need Plus. Recording backup, once it is available, will be free too.

## What Plus adds

- Keep adding letters to every book, as many as you like.

A letter counts when you keep it, spoken or typed, in the book or private. Only your own letters count; your co-parent's never do. Drafts, takes you do not keep, quiet-day marks and edits do not count, and a letter you delete still counts. The 2 free letters are one allowance across all your books, not 2 per book. The full counting rules are in the Terms of Service, section 8.1.

## Plans

| Plan | Price | Billed | Free trial for new subscribers |
|---|---|---|---|
| Plus Monthly | US $4.99 | Every month | The first month free |
| Plus Annual | US $49.99 | Every year | The first 2 months free |

Apple allows one free trial per person for Plus, across both plans. Switching plans does not give you a second trial, and if you have already had one, Apple shows the price with no trial. Plus is offered in the United States and needs iOS 17 or later.

## Automatic renewal

Your plan renews automatically at the end of each period, and at the end of a free trial, at the price shown when you subscribed, until you cancel. Apple charges your Apple Account at the end of the free trial, then at the start of each month or year. With no trial, you are charged when you confirm the purchase.

We never see your card details or receipts. Your plan is checked with Apple on your phone, and our servers do not receive it.

## How to cancel

- On your iPhone: Settings, tap your name, Subscriptions, Early Letters, Cancel Subscription.
- In Early Letters: Settings, Plan, Manage subscription. This opens Apple's subscription screen directly.

Cancel at least 24 hours before your free trial or current period ends to avoid the next charge. Plus keeps working until then. Deleting the app or your account does not cancel your plan.

## Receipts and reminders

- **Apple** sends its own receipts and subscription emails to your Apple Account. Apple's receipt shows the amount you were charged.
- **The app**, on an iPhone signed in to the Apple Account that subscribed, tries to remind you before a free trial ends, before an annual plan renews, and once a year for every plan, with the date and how to cancel. The reminders do not state the price: Apple's subscription screen shows it before you subscribe, and Apple's receipt has it. Reminders come as notifications if you allow them, and as a note in the app the next time you open it. If you have already cancelled, there is nothing to do.
- **Right after you subscribe**, the app shows what you agreed to, with a button to save a copy for yourself. Settings, Plan always shows when your trial ends or your plan renews.
- **We do not email you about Plus yet.** Before we start, we will update these terms. Those emails will go only to the email address of a signed-in account, for a plan bought with that person's own Apple Account, and will state the plan, its price and how often you are charged, the date your trial ends or your plan renews, the last day to cancel and how to cancel. If you have already cancelled, there is nothing to do.

Reminders can arrive late or not at all, for example if the app is deleted or notifications are off, and the app cannot see a cancellation made in your Apple Account until you next open it. Apple keeps billing until you cancel. The dates in Settings, Plan and in Apple's receipts are the ones to rely on.

## Price changes

We may change the price of Plus for new subscribers. While your subscription continues, you keep the price you already pay. We will not renew a plan you have at a higher price. Apple tells you about any price increase and asks for your agreement where its rules require.

## Family Sharing

Family Sharing is on. People in your Apple family group, up to five others, get Plus too, including a co-parent in that group. A co-parent outside your Apple family group needs their own plan. You can turn off sharing for your subscription in your Apple Account settings. Only the person who subscribed gets reminders.

## Offer codes

We may give some people an Apple offer code for free time on Plus. You redeem it with Apple, which shows what happens when the free time ends: either Plus ends with no charge, or it renews at the standard price unless you cancel. Each code says which.

## Refunds

Apple decides refunds. Ask at reportaproblem.apple.com, or in Early Letters under Settings, Plan, Request a refund. Unless the law or Apple's policy says otherwise, there are no partial refunds for unused time. A refund never changes your letters, recordings or books.

## If Plus ends

Your books, letters and recordings stay, to read, play and export. Keeping another letter needs Plus again.
