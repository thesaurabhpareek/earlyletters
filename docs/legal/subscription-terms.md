# Early Letters Plus: Subscription Terms

This is the short summary of Plus. It is part of the Early Letters Terms of Service (https://earlyletters.com/terms), Sections 13 and 14.

## What stays free

- **Your first 2 letters.** The first 2 letters you keep are free. A letter counts when you wrote it, its words are there, and you keep it from Review. Your co-parent's letters never count toward yours. Deleting a letter does not give a free letter back.
- **Every letter you write stays yours to read, play and export, with or without Plus.** If you never subscribe, or Plus ends later, nothing you already kept is locked, hidden or deleted.
- **Always free:** speaking, recording and typing letters, reviewing them, reading and playing them, Read together, starting a book for another child, exporting everything, deleting anything, asking for a copy of your data, and writing the book with your co-parent.
- **A letter that waits.** If you make a letter after your free letters and do not have Plus, it waits on your phone (and, if you are signed in, in your account where only you can see it) until you keep it with Plus or delete it. It is never lost because you did not subscribe.

## What Plus adds

Plus is the membership that lets you keep new letters after your first 2. Everything above stays free without it.

## Automatic renewal

- **Your plan renews automatically** at the end of each period, and at the end of your free trial, at the price shown when you subscribed, until you cancel.
- **When you are charged:** at the end of your free trial, then at the start of each new month or year. With no free trial, you are charged when you confirm the purchase.
- **Who charges you:** Apple, through your Apple Account. Apple handles every payment, renewal, cancellation and refund. We never see your card details, receipts or the price you paid.
- **Your agreement:** when you subscribe in the app, we keep a record of what you agreed to and when: the plan, the kind of offer, the version of these terms you were shown, and the time. If you redeem an offer code outside the app, the record says these terms were not shown to you in the app.

## How to cancel

Cancel at any time:
- **On your iPhone:** Settings, tap your name, Subscriptions, Early Letters, Cancel Subscription.
- **In Early Letters:** Settings, Plan, Manage subscription. This opens Apple's own subscription screen, with nothing in between.

To avoid the next charge, cancel at least 24 hours before your free trial or current period ends. Plus keeps working until then. Deleting the app or your account does not cancel your plan.

## Receipts and reminders

- **Apple** sends its own receipts and subscription messages to your Apple Account.
- **Right after you subscribe**, the app shows what you agreed to, with a button to save a copy for yourself. If you are signed in, we also email you a confirmation.
- **We email you**, if you bought Plus yourself and are signed in with an email address:
  - when your free trial or plan starts, with the date it ends or renews, the date to cancel by, and the price after;
  - before a free trial ends: about a week before for a free trial of a month or less; for a longer free trial, also 16 to 21 days before; and again 4 to 5 days before every free trial ends;
  - about 30 days and again about 7 days before an annual plan renews;
  - once a year for a monthly plan;
  - when your plan is cancelled or ends.
  Each reminder gives the date, the price after and how to cancel.
- **The app** sends the same reminders as notifications on the iPhone that subscribed, if you allow them, and shows a note in the app the next time you open it. Settings, Plan always shows when your trial ends or your plan renews.
- **If you are not signed in,** we have no email address for you, so reminders come only on your phone.
- **If you already cancelled.** We may not see a cancellation you made in your Apple Account until you next open the app, so a reminder can arrive after you cancelled. If so, there is nothing to do.
- **Family Sharing.** Only the person who bought Plus gets our reminder emails.

## Price changes

We may change the price of Plus for new subscribers. While your subscription continues, you keep the price you already pay. We will not renew a plan you have at a higher price.

## Offer codes

- We may give some people an offer code. Codes come from Apple and are redeemed through Apple: in Early Letters under Settings, Plan, Redeem a code, or on Apple's own redemption page. We do not run a code system of our own.
- A code gives the free or reduced period Apple shows when you redeem it, one per offer per person, as Apple decides.
- When a free period from a code ends, your plan renews at the normal price shown above unless you cancel at least 24 hours before it ends. We remind you before it ends, in the same way as for a free trial.
- A code does not change what stays free.

## Sharing Plus with your family

Plus belongs to the Apple Account that subscribed and works on any iPhone signed in to it. Apple Family Sharing is on, so the people in your Apple family group, including a co-parent in that group, get Plus too. A co-parent outside your Apple family group needs their own subscription to keep new letters after their own first 2.

## Gifts

Gifts of Plus are not available yet. When they are, a gift of a year of Plus is paid once and never renews or charges anyone again.

## Refunds

Apple handles refunds: reportaproblem.apple.com, or Settings, Plan, Request a refund in Early Letters, which opens Apple's refund screen. Unless the law or Apple's policy says otherwise, there are no partial refunds for unused time. A refund never changes your letters, recordings or books.

## If Plus ends

Every letter and recording you already kept stays yours to read, play and export, and you can still delete anything. Everything in "What stays free" stays free. Keeping new letters after your first 2 needs Plus again. We never delete a letter or recording because Plus ended.

## If you delete your account

Deleting your account does not cancel Plus; cancel it in your Apple Account. If a subscription you bought is still active when your account is deleted, we keep only your email address, plan and next renewal date until that subscription ends, so we can still send the reminders the law requires, and then delete them. The record of what you agreed to is kept for 3 years after deletion, without your name or email.
