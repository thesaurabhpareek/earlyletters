# Early Letters Plus: Subscription Terms

This is the short summary of Plus. It is part of the Early Letters Terms of Service (https://earlyletters.com/terms), section 6. Apple sells and bills Plus, so Apple's subscription screen shows the exact price and dates before you subscribe, and those are the terms that apply to you.

## What is free

Your first 2 letters. A letter is one saved entry, spoken or typed. You can always read your letters, play your recordings, export everything and delete, with or without Plus.

## What Plus adds

Plus lets you keep adding new letters after your first 2.

If you never subscribe, or Plus ends, every letter and recording you already made stays yours to read, play and export.

## Plans

| Plan | Price | Billed | Free trial for new subscribers |
|---|---|---|---|
| Plus Monthly | US $3.99 | Every month | 1 month |
| Plus Annual | US $29.99 | Every year | 2 months |

One free trial per person, as Apple decides. If you are not eligible, no trial is shown. Plus is offered in the United States.

## Automatic renewal

- Your plan renews automatically at the end of each period, and at the end of a free trial, at the price shown when you subscribed, until you cancel.
- Apple charges your Apple Account: at the end of the free trial, then at the start of each month or year. With no trial, you are charged when you confirm the purchase.
- We never see your card details and we do not receive your purchase records.

## How to cancel

- **On your iPhone:** Settings, tap your name, Subscriptions, Early Letters, Cancel Subscription.
- **In Early Letters:** Settings, Plan, Manage subscription. This opens Apple's own subscription screen.

To avoid the next charge, cancel at least 24 hours before your free trial or current period ends. Plus keeps working until then. Deleting the app does not cancel your plan.

## Dates, receipts and price changes

Settings, Plan shows when your trial ends or your plan renews. Apple sends its own receipts, and Apple tells you about any price change and asks for your agreement where its rules require.

## Family Sharing

Family Sharing is on. People in your Apple family group get Plus too, on their own phones.

## Offer codes

Early testers may be given an Apple offer code for free months. You redeem it with Apple, and Apple shows what happens when the free period ends.

## Refunds

Apple decides refunds: reportaproblem.apple.com, or Settings, Plan, Request a refund in Early Letters. A refund never changes your letters, recordings or books.

## If Plus ends

Your books, letters and recordings stay. Adding new letters needs Plus again.
