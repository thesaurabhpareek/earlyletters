# Early Letters Plus: Subscription Terms

Version 3.1.0. Effective [effective date].

These terms are part of the Early Letters Terms of Service (https://earlyletters.com/terms). Apple sells and bills Plus. Apple's subscription screen shows the exact price and dates before you subscribe, and those are the terms that apply to you.

## What is free

Your first 2 letters. A letter counts when it is your own, its words are on screen, and you keep it. Your co-parent's letters never count toward yours. Recording, typing, reading, playing recordings, reading together, a book for each child, writing with your co-parent, recording backup, export and deletion are always free.

## What Plus adds

Plus lets you keep adding new letters after your first 2, in every book. Every letter you keep stays yours to read, play and export, with or without Plus.

## Plans

| Plan | Price | Billed | Free trial for new subscribers |
|---|---|---|---|
| Plus Monthly | US $4.99 | Every month | The first month free |
| Plus Annual | US $49.99 | Every year | The first 2 months free |

Apple allows one free trial per person for Plus, across both plans. Switching plans does not give you a second trial, and if you have already had one, Apple shows the price with no trial. Plus is offered in the United States and needs iOS 17 or later.

## Automatic renewal

Your plan renews automatically at the end of each period, and at the end of a free trial, at the price shown when you subscribed, until you cancel. Apple charges your Apple Account at the end of the free trial, then at the start of each month or year. With no trial, you are charged when you confirm the purchase.

We never see your card details or receipts. If you have an account, the app tells us your plan, trial end date, renewal date and whether you have cancelled, so we can send the reminders below.

## How to cancel

- On your iPhone: Settings, tap your name, Subscriptions, Early Letters, Cancel Subscription.
- In Early Letters: Settings, Plan, Manage subscription. This opens Apple's subscription screen directly.

Cancel at least 24 hours before your free trial or current period ends to avoid the next charge. Plus keeps working until then. Deleting the app or your account does not cancel your plan.

## Dates and reminders

Settings, Plan always shows when your trial ends or your plan renews, and your Apple Account holds Apple's record of every purchase. Right after you subscribe, the app shows what you agreed to, with a button to save a copy.

We also remind you before a free trial ends, before a yearly plan renews, and once a year on a monthly plan: on the iPhone you subscribed with, as a notification if you allow them and as a note in the app, and by email if you have an account. Emails include your plan's price. If you have already cancelled, there is nothing to do.

Reminders can arrive late or not at all, for example if the app is deleted or notifications are off, and we cannot see a cancellation made in your Apple Account until you next open the app. The dates in Settings, Plan and your Apple Account are the ones to rely on.

Apple tells you about any price increase and asks for your agreement where its rules require. We will not charge you a higher price for a plan you already have unless you agree first.

## Family Sharing

Family Sharing is on. People in your Apple family group, up to five others, get Plus too, including a co-parent in that group. A co-parent outside your Apple family group needs their own plan. You can turn off sharing for your subscription in your Apple Account settings. Only the person who subscribed gets reminders.

## Offer codes

We may give some people an Apple offer code for free time on Plus. You redeem it with Apple, which shows what happens when the free time ends: either Plus ends with no charge, or it renews at the standard price unless you cancel. Each code says which.

## Refunds

Apple decides refunds. Ask at reportaproblem.apple.com, or in Early Letters under Settings, Plan, Request a refund. Unless the law or Apple's policy says otherwise, there are no partial refunds for unused time. A refund never changes your letters, recordings or books.

## If Plus ends

Your books, letters, recordings and backup stay. Keeping another new letter needs Plus again.
