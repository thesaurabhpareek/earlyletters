# Early Letters Plus: Subscription Terms

This is the short summary shown before you subscribe. It is part of the Early Letters Terms of Service ({TERMS_URL}), Section 14.

## What is free

- **Your first 2 letters.** A letter is one saved entry, spoken or typed. After your first 2 letters, adding a new letter needs Plus.
- **Letters you already made always stay.** If you never subscribe, or Plus ends later, every letter and recording you already made stays yours to read, play and export.
- **Export, any time.** Exporting everything is free. It works on your phone without a connection.
- **Deleting and data rights.** Deleting your letters, your book or your account, and asking for a copy of your data, are free and never need Plus.
- **Family authors.** One membership covers the book. Family authors add letters without their own membership.

**[COUNSEL REVIEW, founder decision of 4 Oct 2026: this section replaces "What stays free", which said writing, reading, playing recordings and export were "free, always". Writing new letters is no longer free beyond the first 2. The promise that existing letters stay readable, playable and exportable is kept and is meant to be at least as strong as before. ## What Plus adds

Plus is the membership that unlocks the product. With Plus you can add new letters after your first 2. Plus also includes:

- Encrypted backup of every recording.
- Read together, after the free sessions (3 today; the app shows the current number).
- Books for more than one child. What a second child's book gets without Plus is not decided yet (see Open questions).
- Extra themes and book covers.

One Plus membership covers the book, including for the other co-parent and for family authors who add letters.

## Automatic renewal

- **Your plan renews automatically** at the end of each period, and at the end of your free trial, at the price shown above, until you cancel.
- **When you are charged:** at the end of your free trial, then at the start of each new month or year. With no free trial, you are charged when you confirm the purchase.
- **Who charges you:** Apple, through your Apple Account (Google Play, once the Android app is available). We never see your card details.
- **Your agreement:** we keep a record of what you agreed to and when, and send you a copy by email.

## How to cancel

Cancel at any time:
- **iPhone:** Settings, tap your name, Subscriptions, Early Letters, Cancel Subscription. Or in Early Letters: Settings, Plan, Manage subscription.
- **Android (once available):** Google Play, Payments and subscriptions, Subscriptions, Early Letters, Cancel subscription. Or in Early Letters: Settings, Plan, Manage subscription.

To avoid the next charge, cancel at least 24 hours before your free trial or current period ends. Plus keeps working until then. Deleting the app or your account does not cancel your plan. We never put an offer or extra step between you and cancelling.

## Reminders from us

We tell you by email and in the app:
- when your free trial starts, with the date it ends, the date to cancel by, and the price after;
- at least 3 days before the last day to cancel a free trial; for a free trial longer than one month, also 16 to 21 days before it ends;
- about 30 days before an annual plan renews, and again about 7 days before;
- at least once a year for any plan;
- 7 to 30 days before any price change, with how to cancel. We never raise your price unless you agree.

## Offer codes

- We may give offer codes to some people, such as early testers, neighbours and friends. Codes are issued by us and redeemed through the App Store (Settings, Plan, Redeem a code, or the App Store's own redemption link). We do not run a code system of our own.
- A code gives the free or discounted period shown when you redeem it.
- One code per offer per person, as the store decides.
- When a free period ends, your plan renews at the price shown, unless you turn off renewal before the period ends. You can do that in Settings, tap your name, Subscriptions, Early Letters. We remind you before it ends.
- A free period from a code does not change what is free. Letters you already made always stay readable, playable and exportable.

## Gifts

Gifts of Plus are not available yet. When they are, a gift of a year of Plus is paid once and never renews or charges anyone again.

## Refunds

Apple handles refunds for App Store purchases: reportaproblem.apple.com, or Settings, Plan, Request a refund in Early Letters. For Google Play purchases (once the Android app is available), ask Google or contact us at {SUPPORT_EMAIL}. Unless the law or the store's policy says otherwise, there are no partial refunds for unused time. A refund never changes your letters, recordings or backups.

## If Plus ends

Every letter and recording you already made stays. You can still read, play and export all of them, and you can still delete anything. Recordings already backed up stay stored and downloadable. Adding new letters needs Plus again. We never delete a letter or recording because Plus ended.
