## Verdict: Not ready

The why, the who, the problem and the per-feature thinking are at or above the founder's bar: honest labels, real unhappy paths, testable criteria with numbers, and market tables that say Match, Avoid or Innovate with a reason. The PRD is not ready for 20 agents to build v1.0 because the build list does not match the specs. `08-delivery.md` section 6.3 is "the v1.0 list" and says everything else in the specs' section 12 is built, device work, cut or 1.0.x. That is false for dozens of not-built P0 work packages, including the whole of F07 and F03's languages step (R2P-01). The cut list also removes work that P0 requirements still demand (R2P-02). An agent reading its spec and an agent reading its stream row will build different products, and the 11 Jan date rests on a list that leaves out real work. Both are fixable by the PM lane in a day or two with one reconciliation table. Wave 1 items (the R-17 security batch, the five ADRs, BL-342, the M17 ledger, the counsel delta) are well specified and are not blocked by these findings, once R2P-03 and R2P-06 are settled.

## What is genuinely strong

- **The problem and the customer are grounded and honest.** 01 and 02 separate fact, signal and assumption, say there are no interviews yet, and name the unknowns (U1 to U9) with a study for each. The anti-personas ("parents who want the app to write for them ... we accept losing them") show a real product point of view.
- **The constitution is turned into testable engineering.** F06's edit-type table, the R-01 defect inventory re-run against code on 13 Oct, byte-for-byte replay (F06-REQ-025) and "typed text is never machine-edited" with a byte-equality test are the best part of the PRD.
- **Durability is specified the way a keepsake needs.** F04's draft-before-first-byte rule, 500 kill-during-save runs, the `discarding` state, finalize on interruption, and the VoiceOver take layout that keeps synthetic speech out of a parent's recording (F04 6.2 H) are rare care.
- **Hard calls are made in writing, with a rule for later.** The hard-paywall framing in F14, the pre-committed allowance and price rules (F14 6.9), the removal of "ship free, add Plus later", and M16's plain recovery-key copy all choose honesty over a flattering story.
- **Unhappy paths cover real life.** NICU and loss ("Quiet this book"), separated parents, whisper over white noise, an Arabic letter that starts with a Latin name, a co-parent on Android: these are the cases a Calm or Day One team would test.
- **Market references take positions.** Each feature says Match, Avoid or Innovate with a reason, marks Trustpilot and vendor case studies for what they are, and admits where a rival is ahead (Dearest's privacy label, Tiny Treasures' family plan).
- **The voice is plain.** The lint is clean, sentences are short, and copy proposals follow `VOICE.md` (no counts, no fear, never "AI writes").
