## Verdict: Ready with fixes

Wave 1 can keep running. The streams it dispatched (security gate, contracts, ledger, harnesses, pure core) are specified well enough to build and their claims about the code hold.

The backup chain is **not ready** to build past WP-F08-B1. Two P0 findings sit at the centre of M16: the copy says every use of the recovery key is logged, and the system as specified cannot prove that (R2A-01); and the escrow private key has three different custody stories and is readable by every Edge Function in the project (R2A-02). Both must be settled in the owner-backup ADR at T15, before WP-F08-B2 starts. Five P1 findings in deletion (R2A-03, -04, -05, -16, -17) mean the account deletion ADR at T15 must also widen its matrix before WP-F17-17 and WP-F08-B3p start. Several gates in `06-nfr.md` cannot pass as written (R2A-11, -12, -14, -19, -22); each needs a one-line decision, not new work.

Counts: 2 P0, 17 P1, 10 P2. 25 new (several follow up a round 1 item whose fix opened a new gap), 4 repeat.

## Strengths

- Code claims are honest and checkable. Every "built" or "not built" I checked against `origin/develop` held: four EAS profiles with the switch off, the empty `TRUSTED_SIGNING_KEYS`, `entry-audio` at `since: 'v1.1'`, the ledger counting every `kind = 'letter'` row, the microphone string, `sync_push` as security invoker and `sync_pull` as definer with the `xid8` watermark, the unapplied migrations.
- The sync protocol section in F16 is now the built protocol, with a clear words-group compare-and-set delta, a digest for removals that leaks nothing about private letters, restore epochs and N-1 versioning. Agents can build WP-F16-14 to -16 from it.
- F08 6.5 is a real design: per (child, author) keys, a device key pair per install, self-describing segmented objects with the original's hash inside the ciphertext, an only-in-backup check before any server delete, and a quota. Round 1's two-parent break and data-loss paths are closed.
- The deletion matrix in F17 8.4 names systems, steps, owners and tests, with named exceptions that appear on the deletion screen and in the Policy. It is the right shape; it needs the rows in R2A-03, -04, -05, -16 and -17.
- 08 is candid about confidence (about 50% for T36), names the six dependencies the date rests on, and gives a merge budget with arithmetic. The fenced paths and never-delegated list keep server, auth, payments and claims with the founder.
- Kill switches have a direction model (up, down, kill, variant), signed documents with two keys, and a staleness rule against replayed configs.
- Claims discipline is visible across specs: no "end-to-end", no "only your family's keys", an explicit list of banned backup phrases, and a requirement that the purpose string is true for every user.
