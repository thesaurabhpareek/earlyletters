# R1. Product leader review of PRD V2

Reviewer: independent VP of Product (family subscription apps). Date: 13 Oct 2026. Scope read: `README.md`, `_AUTHORING.md`, `01` to `05`, `08`, `09`, sections 1 to 7 of every `features/F*.md`, spot checks of `07`, `research/R3`. No other file was changed.

## Verdict

The problem is real and the thesis is sharp: parents fail at baby books because of minutes and hands, the category is drifting to AI rewriting, and "your words, exactly as you said them, in your voice" is an open and ownable position. The evidence work is unusually honest (labels, counts, "no primary interviews yet"), the constitution is clear enough to settle arguments, and the acceptance criteria in F04, F06 and F13 are the best I have seen handed to engineers: numeric, observable, with kill tests and property tests. That is the good news. The bad news is that V2 is a careful description of roughly two and a half times what this team can merge by 23 Nov (250 work packages against about 100 merge slots, `08` section 6.1), with every one of 21 features marked P0, so the stack rank never decides anything. The membership mechanics work against the wedge: a new parent can spend both free letters before the 574 MB model has arrived, so the first paywall can come before they have ever seen a faithful transcript. Plus contents, co-parent coverage and the held-letter promise contradict each other across files, and the plan runs on four calendars at once. The north star and the Plus funnel are mostly invisible under opt-in analytics and the gate itself. Do not dispatch 20 agents on this until the five changes at the end are made; most are a day of PM work, not a rewrite.

## The five changes I would make first

1. **Cut v1.0 to fit the merge budget, by rank.** Move owner backup to 1.0.x now (08 option B), decide backup is free and remove it from Plus, and publish a v1.0 WP list of about 100 that the stack rank actually orders (PL-01, PL-02, PL-11).
2. **Fix the membership mechanics before a line of gate code changes.** Count a letter only once its words exist and the author has seen Review; decide DR-16 as B; sync held drafts for signed-in people; delete the "ship free" StoreKit cut; write the pre-committed allowance rule (PL-05, PL-06, PL-08, PL-09, PL-22).
3. **One calendar and a current F02.** All dates in `08` 2.1 only, linted; reconcile F02 to M14 with BL-347 and BL-330 before S01 starts (PL-03, PL-04).
4. **Metrics that survive opt-in and the gate.** An ASC-only paying-families metric as the business number, "letters made" versus "letters kept", WKF by plan state, and the unmeasurable activation target dropped (PL-15, PL-16, PL-17).
5. **Get the two existential answers and a way to reach parents.** Run Study 2 this month, put Study 1 inside C1 with an owner, write a two-channel acquisition plan, and narrow the positioning to the visible change list and the languages that pass (PL-12, PL-13, PL-14, PL-25).
