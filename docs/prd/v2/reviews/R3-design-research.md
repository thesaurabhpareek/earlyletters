# R3. Design and research review of PRD V2

Status: 13 Oct 2026. Independent review by a design and user-research critic who did not write the PRD. Read: `README.md`, `_AUTHORING.md`, `02-customers.md`, `03-goals-and-principles.md`, sections 2, 5 and 6 of `features/F01` to `F21`, the accessibility parts of `07-quality-bar.md` and `06-nfr.md`, `research/R1` and `R2` in full where cited, `09-decisions-and-risks.md` DR-12, DR-15, DR-16, `08-delivery.md` section 2, `docs/release/journey/INDEX.md` and `critiques/design.md`, and `docs/design/DESIGN_LANGUAGE.md`. Nothing else was edited. IDs use the prefix DX so they do not clash with decision rows DR-01 to DR-17.

## Verdict

**Not ready to hand to two designers and get the same product back. Ready for engineering on durability; not ready on the moments that decide how a tired parent feels.**

What is strong: capture durability is specified better than most shipped apps (F04 unhappy paths, kill tests with counts, "Everything you said is kept"); the constitution is enforced in the spec, not just stated; due-date silence, names off the lock screen, no streaks and the Recently deleted shelf are the right calls; F06 Review gives a parent proof of every machine change, which no competitor does.

What is not: the money moment (the Keep gate after 2 letters) is designed as a legal and StoreKit flow, not as an emotional one, and the letter it "holds" is less durable than every other letter in the product. The two worst days a parent can have in this app, a child's death or a NICU stay, have no self-serve path; support is a founder inbox with a 2-day reply. Multilingual reading is correct at the glyph level and thin at the human level: a co-parent who cannot read Hindi gets a block of Devanagari, no label, no voice. Twelve specs still carry text that contradicts M2 or M14, so two designers reading different files build different products. And the whole product rests on an untested premise (parents will speak letters aloud near a sleeping baby), with no primary research done and the first study scheduled 16 days before submission.

Counts: 39 findings. P0: 7. P1: 23. P2: 9.
