# Design review 22 Nov 2026: motion, transitions, tokens, component library, accessibility (cross-cutting slice)

Status: DRAFT for the design backlog. Author: `design-systems`. Docs only: no token, component or screen changes. Read against `origin/develop` in a sparse worktree (docs, `packages/design-tokens`, `apps/mobile/src`), 22 Nov 2026. No screen was run on a phone, VoiceOver was not run, no test was run (no `npm install`; disk nearly full). Does not repeat PRs #1566 and #1567.

Evidence tags: `[verified: file:line]` I read the line or ran a grep on it this session. `[unverified]` inferred from a doc or a pattern, needs a device or a closer read.

## 1. Headline

The token discipline is better than the docs suggest. No hex or rgb colour appears in any screen or component outside comments (grep of `apps/mobile/src`, tests and `*.data.ts` excluded). Every duration and spring in the motion layer comes from `tokens.motion` except the handful listed below. The gaps are: a few raw numbers, doc drift (MOTION.md, COMPONENTS.md), a missing proof that accessibility holds on a device, and several things only a device can settle. Review A on 22 Nov withdrew two of my first findings (DSR-002, DSR-012); they stay listed as checked, not a gap, and every cite was re-read on `origin/develop` after a merge.
