## 1. The walk, in his words

My partner texts me a link and says "put your letters in too". I am on a train. I do not install apps. I tap the link. If I am lucky it opens a plain page that says "Someone invited you to share a book" and "This invite opens in the app on an iPhone". It does not say who, which book, what it costs, or what she will be able to read of mine. I have to trust her text. Fine, I trust her. I tap "Copy the invite", get sent to the store, install, open it, and I have to remember to tap a small quiet "I was invited" under the big "Begin the book" button. That is three steps I have to hold in my head across two apps.

Then I am asked my age, then to sign in, then to agree to sync. Only after that does it tell me who invited me, with her sign-off ("Invited by Mama"). It says "You will both read every letter in the book. Join only if you know who sent you this link." Good for a stranger. For me it raises the thing I actually want to know: does she read everything I record? It does not say. I tap Join anyway.

It tells me I am in the book, shows the cover with my kid's name, and asks what the child calls me. I pick Papa. I land on the Book and see her letters, each signed "From Mama". Mine will sit beside hers, it says. I tap Speak on the commute home. The screen says "Only you, until you add it to the book." That is the one line in the whole app that tells me where my words go, and it is a good one. I talk for a minute. I read the words. I tap "Add to the book". Nothing tells me she will now read it, that she cannot change it, or that she will not hear my voice, only read my words. I find out she cannot hear it only if I open one of her letters and see "Recording kept on Mama's phone" and wonder if mine says the same on her side.

I look for a way out, or a place that says my letters are mine. There is no Leave button in this build. There is no page that says "what each of us can see and change". If I think about my partner's plan versus mine, nothing explains that either. I would probably still write the letter. But I would write it with a small worry that I cannot settle from inside the app.

## 2. What already works (so nobody "fixes" it)

- The invite never joins by itself: peek, then a tap on "Join the book" (`apps/mobile/src/app/invite/index.tsx:142-161`, `:272-294`). Before sign-in the sheet knows and shows nothing about the book (`:306-312`).
- The child's name appears only after joining (`family.en.ts:87`, `invite/index.tsx:415`). The signature is asked with her suggestion prefilled (`:431-452`).
- Free letters count only his own letters, so joining her 30-letter book does not bring his paywall closer (`apps/mobile/src/lib/db/repos/ledger.ts:34-58`, M17, F11-REQ-025).
- Listen says plainly that a take is private until added (`listen.tsx:547`, `strings.en.ts:295`).
- On the letter page her letter is signed with her sign-off, no edit control for him (`letter/[id].tsx:336`, `:339-346`).
- The marketing film is a letter From Papa (`apps/web/src/content/site.ts:35`, `:186`). A father is shown as the author, not as a helper.

## 4. Founder decisions raised

1. PER2-002 and PER2-003 (P1 only for an alpha inviter with a store-build father): what a store build says when an invite link or the `/j` page reaches someone who cannot join yet. D-126, D-161 and D-162 say no "coming soon" surface in any build, so either line is a founder-carved exception. The option that needs no exception is to make the `/j` page and the share message stop pointing at a button the store build lacks; `/j` is web copy, so D-161 may not bind it. The cost is a father who is told to tap a button that does not exist.
2. PER2-006, PER2-008, PER2-011: three short lines about what the other parent can and cannot do, and that his recording stays on his phone. They are promises about what users get, so they are the founder's under CR-73. All three are now P2 (text true, alpha-only). PER2-011 also needs the D-113 check and PER2-017's answer before any line is promised.
3. PER2-013: DR-16, whether a book's Plus covers the other parent outside Apple Family Sharing.
