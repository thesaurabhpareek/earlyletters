## 1. Her morning, in her own words

My daughter has put a baby book on her phone. She says I can read it. I have my glasses on and my phone set to the very biggest letters, because otherwise I cannot read my own text messages.

(Imagined, not a path v1.0 can produce: the app only creates co-parent invites, so my daughter cannot send me a family link today. If an older build or another source ever did, I think I would meet "That link doesn't open an invite." and not know what I did wrong. See PER3-001 and 002, P2.) What really happens on day one is simpler: she hands me her phone.

On her phone, the book opens on a screen that says good morning to her, with her name. There is a big Speak button and a Type button. I only want to read. I find the Book at the bottom. The words are huge, which is what I wanted. At the top is a heading with her baby's name and a "Months" button. I tap a letter. The letter opens, and I can read it, but "Back" at the top may have grown so large that it sits on the first line [estimate]. I scroll down, past all the words, and at the very end I find a small round Play button. I press it. I hear my daughter's voice saying what she said last spring. I am crying a little.

Then I press "Read together". It starts at the very first letter, not the one I was looking at. The writing may be so big that I see only a few letters of a word at a time [estimate], and I cannot make it smaller. Two enormous buttons sit at the bottom, and between them and the top of the screen there may be room for only one or two lines [estimate]. I want to say something to the baby myself. I tap Type and write. It says nothing about who I am. When I look at the Book, my words are signed "From Mama".

I do not know who to ask for help. There is nothing on these screens that says Help.

## 3. What worked, so nobody breaks it

- Large type is honoured in the letter itself: letter text has no cap (D-027), a `readingMeasure` lifts the column width at 1.6x and up, and no `numberOfLines` truncates a letter [verified: packages/design-tokens/src/tokens.ts:303-310; DSR-023].
- Read together stacks its two buttons at accessibility sizes rather than shrinking them [verified: turn-controls.logic.ts:1-15], and the page-turn has a swipe as well as buttons.
- The Months sheet lists only months that have letters and says "Go to a month" [verified: apps/mobile/src/components/book/copy.ts:19-21]. It does not count gaps.
- Delete asks first with "Keep it" as the default, and the letter waits 30 days [verified: letter/[id].tsx:388].
- No internal term appears in the strings quoted in this review (take, entry, draft, pack); I did not grep every string the three screens use. "Small fixes" and "Exactly as said" are approved glossary terms.

## 4. Founder decisions this review surfaces

1. Does "each letter signed" (BRAND.md:40) hold on a phone that is handed over? If yes, PER3-004 is a promise break and moves to P0, and a "Who is this from?" step ships before submission. If no, say so in the BRAND note so the store text does not overpromise.
2. Do we name the update that brings grandparents (PER3-001, PER3-013), or keep the line without a date? Public promise, so the founder's.
3. Is a "hand over to read" mode in v1.0 scope (PER3-003)? It is the honest answer to how a grandmother reads on day one, since her own door is closed until v1.1.
4. What does a guest see at Keep and at Plus on a handed-over phone (PER3-018)? It touches who is shown an offer. Founder or CPO.
5. May Read together stop stacking Large print on the system size (PER3-005)? It overrides the S30 spec, so it needs a ruling from design or the founder.
