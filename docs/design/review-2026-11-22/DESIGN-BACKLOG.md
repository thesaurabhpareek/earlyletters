## How to read this

- One ticket can close several findings. Every source id is cited, with the final severity from its own file (not an earlier draft). A ticket carries the highest severity among its ids. Withdrawn ids (CDO-A-010, CDO-A-019) are not sources of any ticket; see section 10.
- Order: the cut line and the ready-now list first; then the founder questions; then the two proof gaps by instruction (section 1); then the one customer P0, the first letter, the three persona rows a parent feels first and the gaps that sit beside it (section 2); then customer-facing P1s that are live today (section 3); then P1s that are dark behind the Moments flag (section 4); then internal re-syncs and design-system work (section 5); then smaller fixes (section 6); then the remaining unreviewed areas (section 7). - Lanes: `design-systems` is tokens, motion and transitions, components and accessibility rules. `mobile` builds. `content` owns words. `product` owns flow, scope and screen decisions. The reviews used five lanes (design-systems, motion, transition, content, mobile); their screen-design rows land in `product` here and their motion and transition rows in `design-systems`. "Founder, CR-73" in a lane cell means the row changes what users are promised, scope, money or legal text, so the founder rules before anyone builds.
- Sizes: S under a day, M a few days, L a week or more. A ticket that mixed a measurement, a decision and a build is split into those pieces.
- Evidence limits carry over. None of the nine reviews ran a phone, VoiceOver or a test. Every "verified" in them is a file read. Nothing here is a measurement.
