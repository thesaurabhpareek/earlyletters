## 1. Verdict in six lines

1. The promise (words stay yours, original kept, private, yours always) is strong and is mostly carried into the product as built. Do not break it.
2. The time to first letter is the largest risk. A new parent meets an age gate, five first-run steps, a model download ask, Home, a mic primer, and an iOS alert before the first word is recorded. The 90 second target (PRD 03, PS1) is not credible on this path.
3. The tab story is not settled. IA-FINAL says three tabs and no Moments tab; PRD 28 says four, with Moments second; the code has a shell that would show two tabs both labelled "Book" if the flag were turned on today; PR #1567 proposes pictures on Home, which overlaps Moments again. Home, Moments, Book and Me do not yet each have a job a parent could state in one sentence.
4. Me is a 20-row settings list. Privacy and "where my letters live" are spread over five rows. A parent looking for "is this private" has no single answer page.
5. Motion and transitions between the surfaces are designed in pieces (Home, Listen, Review, letter page, Moments) and there is no one document that shows the whole journey, so seams are where the risk sits.
6. What already works is listed in section 5. Protect it.

## 2. Journeys walked

J1 Install to first letter. J2 The first night (2 a.m., one hand). J3 After the first letter (what lands where, which asks come). J4 Week one (Home, Moments, Book, reminders, Plus gate). J5 Finding and trusting the privacy story. J6 Co-parent and sharing. J7 Looking after the account (Me).

## 5. What already works (do not break)

- The promise is built into the product, not only the copy: the promise step shows a live cleaned sample, every fix is marked, and the original recording is kept (`strings.en.ts:81-98`; PRD 03 section 1).
- The microphone is the first permission and it comes in context from Speak, behind a primer, never in first run (`listen.tsx:266-295`; PRD 28 line 659).
- Listen is built for the dark and one hand: no lamp, a single start circle, screen awake only while recording, a take is kept when the app is interrupted (`listen.tsx:1-22`).
- Durability first: the draft is written before the microphone goes live, and the Keep gate opens before anything is saved, so a closed sheet or a kill never loses a letter (`listen.tsx:3-8`; `review.tsx:18-33`).
- One ask at a time after the first letter, in a decided order, and "Not now" always works (`review.tsx:524-530`).
- The Keep gate asks only when the words are on screen (M17) and holds the letter safely (`review.tsx:22-33`).
- Home is built around one prompt and two equal capture buttons, pinned in the thumb zone, with a reduced-motion path (`(tabs)/index.tsx:1-30`).
- Empty states are warm and never count gaps: closed cover on the Book, no "nothing yet" on Home (`IA-FINAL-20nov.md:455-477`).
- The Moments photo permission is never a first-open alert; it has a primer, Apple's sheet, and a door that works when the parent says no (`moments-design-closure.md` DC-1).
- Me keeps "If you are struggling" always present, and a Plan row in every build (`settings/index.tsx:1-30`).
- The tab bar is accessible by design: Large Content Viewer, fixed 13 pt labels, VoiceOver position strings that count visible tabs only (`(tabs)/_layout.tsx:74-78`; `lib/nav/moments-tab.ts:40-60`).
- Plus is honest and early: reading, listening, export and delete are free forever and the welcome says so (`strings.en.ts:75`; PRD 03 principle 6).
