## 2. Verdict in short

The built slice is better disciplined than the paper around it: no hex colours in the slice files except Google's required logo, no raw durations in onboarding, sign-in or Write, Reduce Motion handled by a shared `useMotion`, and the destructive flows (Erase, Delete account) read as carefully thought through. The gaps are mostly at the seams: a type-scale cap that inverts heading and body at AX5 on every sign-in sheet and destructive confirmation; two different "what stays free" promises in app copy; a purchase-confirmation screen that is thinner than its spec; a few dead-end or silent-failure states; and several paper designs (bedtime-anchored reminder setup, the story intro) that are not built and not marked deferred in the design set.
