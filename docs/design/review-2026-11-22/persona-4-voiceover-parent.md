## 1. The walk, in her words

I am Mara. I am blind, my phone talks all day, and my daughter is four months old. I want her first words on record, in my own voice, and I want to be sure it is kept.

First run. A gate asks me to answer a question and tick a box. The two answers are a radio pair, and nothing is picked for me. I can hear "radio group" and move between them. If my finger lands on No and I tap Continue, the app shuts me out for a day. Nobody asks "are you sure". I get through, and the welcome and the child step announce each heading and move my focus to it. The Continue button says it is dimmed and tells me why. That is kind. The birthday is a calendar I have to walk day by day.

Home. I find Speak, and I tap it. The first time, a short note tells me the phone will ask about the microphone, and my focus lands on its heading. Then Apple asks, and I allow it. A screen opens with a circle I cannot see. The line on it says "Tap the red circle and talk". The control I can reach is named "Start recording". I double tap it.

And then nothing. The control I just pressed is gone. I hear no tone and no "listening". The app has decided, on purpose, to say nothing into the take, because my screen reader would be in the recording. I understand why. But I am a mother with a baby on one arm, and I do not know whether I am recording. I wait, and I say my first sentence into what I hope is a live microphone. When I am done I swipe to find Finish, and I hope. I find Pause and Finish. Pause is named the same whether I am recording or paused. I press Finish.

Review. The first thing I land on is a play button, before the page's heading. Then my words, read as one paragraph. A line says there are "2 small fixes", folded shut. I open it. The rows tell me what changed ("Usher to Asha"). I tap a row to see what I said, and a card appears. It is in the letter above me, not under my finger, and nothing tells me it opened. I find "Put it back" by swiping backward, press it, and hear nothing. I am not told it worked. I can hear my own recording by pressing Play, but there is no way to move around in it here.

Keep. I press Keep it. This part is excellent: the screen holds still until the sentence "Kept in Month 4. Added to Asha's book" has been said, then moves on. Then a sheet rises on its own and asks me to keep the book safe with an account. It does not wait for me. I did not ask for it, and I am still taking in where I am.

Later I find the letter in the Book. Month headings are headings, so I can jump by them. The letter page's player has a scrubber I can adjust. Delete is at the bottom, behind a sheet that asks first. After it, I am back on the Book with a toast I may or may not find. Export is a whole-book thing in Settings; there is no share or export on a single letter.

## 3. What worked for her (do not break)

- Capture is built so the app never speaks into a take: one gate, a state machine, a heartbeat, and a test that the gate fails closed (`src/lib/speech-gate.ts:1-60`). Held lines such as "Paused." are spoken once the recorder has really stopped (`src/components/capture/listen.logic.ts:163-195`).
- The saved moment waits for VoiceOver to finish "Kept in Month N" (3 s at least, 8 s at most, and `announcementFinished` on iOS) before moving on (`src/components/capture/saved-moment.tsx:74-129`). That is the clearest "confirm it was kept" in the app.
- The microphone primer and onboarding steps move focus to their heading (`src/app/listen.tsx:631`, `src/app/onboarding.tsx:255`). The dimmed Continue names the reason as its hint (`src/app/onboarding.tsx:361-364`).
- Toasts never leave while a screen reader runs, and an Undo toast never times out (`src/components/ui/toast.tsx:11-14`).
- The tab bar counts only visible tabs for "i of n", and each tab sets a large content title (`src/app/(tabs)/_layout.tsx:55-63`). The labels are Home, Book, Me (`packages/content/src/strings.en.ts:61-63`).
- The letter page player has an adjustable scrubber and a value (`src/components/player/audio-player.tsx:173-178`); month openers are headings (`src/components/book/month-opener.tsx:26-29`); the Delete confirm names "Keep it" as the safe primary (`src/components/book/confirm-sheet.tsx`).
- Reduce Motion: the saved moment and Review use fades, and Listen reads Reduce Motion for its aura (`src/app/listen.tsx:113`). I found no case where motion alone carries a meaning for her.

## 4. For `qa`: how to prove these

None of these can be closed from a desk. They need one device run by a person using VoiceOver, with and without headphones, and then Voice Control for PER4-004. Proposed rows for `docs/qa/DEVICE_TEST_PLAN.md`: start-recording cue (PER4-001, 002), Review arrival and fix undo (PER4-005, 007, 008), after-Keep ask (PER4-011), sheet focus return (PER4-012), birthday picker (PER4-017). A test that every live-region-only change also calls `announce()` (PER4-018) is the one piece that can be written now.
