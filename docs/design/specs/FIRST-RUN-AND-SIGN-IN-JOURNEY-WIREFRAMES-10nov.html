4. Returning user: signed in (no screen), and the app lock cover

Signed in, lock off: the app opens on Tonight, nothing in between. Signed in, lock on: the cover is drawn at once, the system sheet asks once, a failed or cancelled ask leaves the lock screen with Unlock. The lock never asks for an account password and never replaces sign-in recovery.
