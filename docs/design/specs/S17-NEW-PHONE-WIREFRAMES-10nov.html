NX-36-S17 new phone wireframes

Companion to S17-NEW-PHONE-SPEC-10nov.md (10 Nov 2026, draft). Static HTML and CSS, no scripts, no images, no external requests. Colours are the token hex values from packages/design-tokens/src/tokens.ts, as in the NX-36 wireframes. Typefaces and the line drawing are stand-ins; the AX5 frames use an illustrative 2.4 scale. Layout and hierarchy studies, not pixel specs. All words are placeholders that need content and founder approval; the lines that say where recordings are need counsel. No motion is drawn and none is added.
