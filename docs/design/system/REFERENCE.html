Design system v2: components

What this is. Every component in COMPONENTS.md (ids N1 to S2) in every state, each drawn three times: on light paper, on dark paper, and on a drawn picture (shapes only). The third tile follows the Dark toggle. Glass is simulated with CSS backdrop-filter and a drawn highlight: use it to judge layout, states, contrast and fallbacks, not Apple's real material. Words are placeholders from the house voice; the area WORDS.md files own the strings. AX5 sizes follow FOUNDATIONS.md 4.3 at 375 pt. The fictional family is Asha. Toggles can also be set from the address: ?dark&rt&hc&rm&ax5.

Motion primitives (MOTION.md)

Each plays the named primitive with its token timing. With Reduce Motion on, each plays its MOTION section 8 substitute. Glass never fades by opacity: it appears by its style (simulated here by blur and tint).

Sources: BRIEF.md section 1 (HIG Materials, Adopting Liquid Glass, Expo SDK 57). Token values: packages/design-tokens/src/tokens.ts and FOUNDATIONS.md sections 2, 3, 4.3, 6. Motion values: MOTION.md section 13a. This page writes nothing, loads nothing, and runs offline.
