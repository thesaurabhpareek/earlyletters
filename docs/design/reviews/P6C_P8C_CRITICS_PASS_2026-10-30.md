## 2. Findings, ranked

### Must before build 2

**M1. After a sole parent requests deletion, the next sync turns the phone back into the welcome, and no discoverable path reaches "Cancel deletion".**
- Spec: S64 Scheduled (line 1619) "Cancel deletion bordered"; `scheduled.body` (KEEP, line 1614) "You can cancel any time before then, here or by signing in again."; `confirm.body` (KEEP, 1612) "Until then, sign in and cancel, and everything comes back as it was."; acceptance (1628) "Deletion completes in the app". The 30-day undo is C-REQ-019 and LEGAL-REQ-029.
- Built: the request tombstones the sole parent's book with the account (`20261005000000_family_cap_language_idempotency.sql:439-442`), and the next pull returns that book as `gone`, reason `deleted` (`20261016000000_sync_v1_server.sql:1618-1621`). The phone sets `server_state = 'deleted'` (`lib/sync/merge.ts:844-846`), and a deleted book is not listed (`lib/db/repos/children.ts:29`). With no listed book Tonight redirects to onboarding (`app/(tabs)/index.tsx:183`), and the Book tab shows the same welcome. The only way into Settings is the gear on the Book tab (`app/(tabs)/book.tsx:265`), so Settings, Account and Delete account (which holds Cancel deletion) are no longer reachable. The request itself starts no sync and leaves no local note that a deletion is open (`lib/account-deletion/use-account-deletion.ts:57-75`). The server's request email also names the path: "Until then, you can sign in and cancel the request in Settings, Delete account." (`packages/content/src/emails/account.en.ts:51`).
- Rendered, with the mocked server above (web, server-on build; not seen on a phone or against staging): Mama, sole parent of Asha's book with six letters, requested deletion (step 1 read "Asha's book will be deleted, with your 6 letters in it."). After the sync the scheduled page was still on screen, but its back arrow led to "The baby memory book you fill by talking. / A few words at a time, in your own voice. Kept for your child to read and hear for years. / Begin the book / I already have a book / I was invited", on Tonight and on the Book tab alike (`del-40-sole-after-back-synced.png`, `del-41-sole-root.png`). The only controls on that screen were those three buttons. "I already have a book" (she is still signed in) opened "No book here yet / Did you use a different way to sign in last time? / Try another way / Start a book" (`del-42-sole-i-already-have.png`); that line comes from the mock's empty membership count, so what the real server's rows would make the sheet say is unverified, but the way to Settings is not there either way. Her six letters were in Recently deleted, "Deleted October 30. Kept here until November 29." (`del-17-recently-deleted-after-request.png`, opened by address in the probe), which she could not open from any screen she could reach.
- A parent: asks for deletion on Friday night, sleeps on it, and on Saturday morning wants it back, as the page told her she could. She opens the app and finds a welcome screen as if she had never written a letter, with no Settings, and no sign that her request is still open. She has no way to cancel in the app, in the first hours of the 30 days that exist so she can. The one run-sheet step that cancels uses a parent whose book stays with a co-parent (AL-07.5, Drill Mama and Papa), and the sole-parent drill (AL-07.7, Drill D) runs to completion without a cancel, so neither would find this. A co-parent's book is not tombstoned: after the same sync Mama's Book showed "The first page is waiting." (two other cards stayed, which the probe did not identify) and her six letters waited in Recently deleted, "Kept here until November 29." (`del-36-coparent-book.png`, `del-35-coparent-shelf.png`), which is what step 1 said ("Your 6 letters leave it."), and Settings stayed reachable.
- Fix (the coordinator routes it; logic is S03's and S14's, P8c owns the screen): either (a) S14 keeps a local note when a request is open (its date, set on the request, cleared on cancel and on a status read that finds none) and the first-run welcome, and any phone with no listed book, shows one calm line and one button, "Your account is set to be deleted on {date}." / "Cancel deletion", that opens the Delete account page (the route is exempt from the no-book redirect); or (b) S03 and S02 pull a book tombstoned by an open request as a listed book (empty, with a marker) and only as `gone` once the purge has run, so Settings stays reachable. (a) is the smaller change and works for a new phone too if the note is read from the server on sign-in. Either way: add a sole-parent step to AL-07 before the freeze (request, force-quit, reopen, find Cancel deletion, cancel, watch the Book come back), and say in the spec's S64 acceptance that a sole parent can reach Cancel deletion after a sync.

- Added after the independent review of this document: one non-obvious route exists, so the wording above says "discoverable". The welcome's "Begin the book" creates a second book (`app/onboarding.tsx:212-228`, no deletion check); once that book is listed, Book > gear > Settings > Delete account > Cancel deletion is reachable. Whether the server accepts a second book while a request is open is not traced here. Fix option (a) has to cover this case too (no "Begin the book" over an open request).

### Should before build 2

**S1. On the launch screen, Try again does nothing she can see when it fails again.**
- Spec: S51 (lines 1226 to 1243): Try again is one of the two actions; no state is given for a second failure.
- Built: `use-launch.ts:13-20` runs `setStatus(attemptOpen(...))`, and `attemptOpen` returns the same string `'recovery'` (`launch.logic.ts:16`, `:25`), so React sees an equal state and does not render. The button only dims while pressed (`plain-screen.tsx` Pressable opacity); there is no busy label, no note.
- Rendered: `?fault=launch`, Try again tapped, 1.5 s watched with a mutation observer: **0 DOM changes**, the same text (`launch-after-tryagain-persistent.png`). With `?fault=launch-once` it works and the Tonight screen follows. On the crash screen, the web remount goes back to Tonight (so the probe could not hold the fault); on a phone `retry` re-renders the same route and the same error, which is the same silence (read only, unverified).
- A parent: at 2 AM taps Try again, sees nothing, taps it again, wonders whether the phone is frozen, and may force-quit in the middle of something. The hint above offers "restart the phone", which is the right advice, but the button she was told to try gives no answer.
- Fix: make a second failure visible and calm. Return an attempt counter or a fresh object so the screen renders, and show one line under the hint, a new key such as `errors.launch.stillClosed`: "It still did not open. Nothing was changed." Keep the busy label brief so a slow open does not look like nothing. Add the state to S51's States line and a j20 step (`?fault=launch`, Try again, the line).

**S2. The launch screen promises safety it cannot check, and its own export line then contradicts it.**
- Spec: `errors.launch.body` (line 1234) "Your letters and recordings are safe on this phone." with the note "`rules.test.ts` requires it in `en.errors`"; `errors.launch.exportNothing` (1239) "This phone has no recordings or files to export."
- Built: both lines are on the same screen (`strings.en.ts:1016-1030`, `launch-recovery.tsx:39`). The claims inventory marks "Your letters are safe on this phone." unverified (A2, "Local storage durability not opened"); on S51 the database has just failed to open, which is the one time the build knows least.
- Rendered: tap Export what's readable and the page reads, in order, "The book could not open / Your letters and recordings are safe on this phone. / Try again, or restart the phone. / This phone has no recordings or files to export." (`launch-export-note.png`). The web has no files, so this is the real state there; on a phone it appears whenever the two folders cannot be read.
- A parent: reads that her letters and recordings are safe, taps the only other button, and is told the phone has no recordings. "No recordings or files" is also a loss sentence (the content rules: no loss language).
- Fix: say what the build can prove. The code never deletes or changes anything on this screen (`use-launch.ts` header, `launch-recovery.tsx` header, the j20 button check), so on S51 use `errors.launch.body` "Nothing on this phone has been deleted or changed." (S50 keeps "safe on this phone", a render error does not touch the database, and `rules.test.ts:271` is still met by `errors.crash.body`), and `exportNothing` "Nothing could be read from this phone just now. Nothing was changed." A spec-owner change (section 5); the content rules and the claims inventory row A2 are the reasons.

- Review note: the spec records review decision B6 (lines 1234 and 1240): `exportFailed` was revised with no "nothing was changed" padding, and the second sentence was removed as padding. The proposed lines above reintroduce that phrasing, so the spec owner rules them against B6 (section 9 item 3); where a line can state what the screen does instead of what it did not do, prefer that.

- Correction after the fix PRs (30 Oct 2026, product agent): the Fix paragraph above says "The code never deletes or changes anything on this screen". That is wrong for Try again. The recovery screen's own code only reads (Export copies the files byte for byte and writes only its own folder and ZIP in the cache), but Try again runs `openStore()` again, which runs the store's update steps (`apps/mobile/src/lib/db/migrations.ts`). Five of those steps write data (v2, v3, v5, v7 and v8), v2 and v3 delete three settings keys, and a step that finished before a failing one stays applied. So "Nothing on this phone has been deleted or changed.", proposed above and in section 5, cannot be shown, and #394 (merged as c74c1b7) did not use it: it worded the launch body about what Export does and made no "nothing was changed" claim (claims inventory section 3f, rows E1 to E4 and E8; E8, the ZIP README, is still unverified). The original text above is left as written. The follow-ups are in `docs/BACKLOG.md`, BL-495.

**S3. "Sign out of this phone" on the scheduled page lands on "Sign in to delete your account."**
- Spec: S64 Scheduled (line 1619): "Cancel deletion bordered, Sign out of this phone"; `signedOut.*` KEEP (1614).
- Built: the quiet button signs out at once, with no confirm (`settings/delete-account.tsx:152`), and the same route then draws the signed-out state, `c.signedOut.body` (`:91`, `account-deletion.en.ts:72`).
- Rendered: after "Sign out of this phone" the page read "Delete account / Sign in to delete your account. Letters that are only on this phone stay here until you delete the app. / Sign in" (`del-57-after-sign-out.png`).
- A parent: has just scheduled a deletion, taps the quiet option beneath it, and is told to sign in to delete the account she just deleted. Nothing says the request is still open or that signing in again is how she cancels it. The Account page's own sign-out confirm says "Your letters stay on this phone. Sign in again any time."; this path has no such line.
- Fix: change the signed-out line to a sentence true in both situations: "Sign in to delete your account, or to cancel a deletion you started. Letters that are only on this phone stay here until you delete the app." (`signedOut.body`, not a registered line.) With M1's local note, a phone that knows a request is open can say the date.

**S4. The page never says what happens to the letters on this phone.**
- Spec: S64 copy (lines 1606 to 1609): `what.intro` "from our servers", `what.copies` "A copy you exported stays wherever you saved it." Nothing about the phone's own copy; the run sheet expects "the letters on the phone are handled as the screen said" (AL-07.3).
- Built: step 1, step 2, the scheduled page and the toast speak only of servers and "your account". The request starts no local change; the next sync moves the person's own letters to Recently deleted (`lib/sync/merge.ts:630-660`, the pull's `deleted_at`).
- Rendered, co-parent, after one sync: Tonight read "What do you want Asha to know about today?", the Book "The first page is waiting.", and Recently deleted listed the six letters "Deleted October 30. Kept here until November 29. / Restore" (`del-37-coparent-tonight.png`, `del-36-coparent-book.png`, `del-35-coparent-shelf.png`). So the letters move to Recently deleted with a date line; they are not erased at the tap. What happens on the phone on that date is not shown here: in a sync build the phone's own launch purge leaves letters the server holds alone (`lib/store.ts:797-808` `purgeExpired`, `lib/db/repos/entries.ts:338-346`, `components/book/shelf.logic.ts:10-13`), and the phone erases a server-held letter only when a later pull says the server no longer holds it (`lib/sync/merge.ts:748-760` `markGone`). Whether the phone gets that signal after the account purge at day 30 (the auth user is gone by then) is unverified; S03 confirms it. Sole parent: see M1.
- A parent: asks herself "will my letters on this phone go?" and the page does not answer. She finds her Book empty the next morning with no warning on any screen she read. Nothing is promised that the build does not do, but the most personal thing, her own phone, is the thing the page is silent about.
- Fix: one sentence on step 1 under the book lines, and one in the cancel toast, true for synced letters: "On this phone, your letters move to Recently deleted, each with its date." (conditional: letters that were never synced stay put, which the signed-out line already says.) S03 confirms the behavior; the line is not registered text. A spec-owner change (section 5).

**S5. The held intro still says "photos", and the proposed line names a backup that build 2 does not have.** (Counsel-gated, C8.)

- A parent: reads a sentence about the one irreversible act in the app that names photos, which v1.0 never makes, and recordings, which build 2 does not upload (backup is off in every profile, `eas.json`). #358 notes the proposed line has the same problem with "recording backup".
