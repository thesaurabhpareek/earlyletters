Area D motion: family, sharing and reminders

Each card plays one item of MOTION.md, keyed by its id (D-Mnn) and the screen ids of DESIGN.md (D-Snn). Turn on Reduce Motion to see each item's reduced form; it starts on if your system asks for reduced motion. The log under each phone lists what happens and when: the commit, the haptic and any sound (Paper sounds are off by default, so sounds are listed only as "with Paper sounds on"). Grey surfaces marked "system" are iOS's; their motion here is a stand-in.

5. Never animate in area D

  • No confetti, burst, sparkle, tick or bounce after a share, an invite or a join.
  • No counter ticking anywhere; no badge on the app icon or a tab.
  • No ringing or wiggling bell, no pulsing Share control, no coach mark.
  • No motion that says time passed or something is missing: back-off is invisible (D-M45).
  • No run or chain between quiet days; no wash on a quiet day (D-M60).
  • No word animates: letter words, typed-day words and notification text are never typed on.
  • No loop where nothing is happening; the one loop (D-M18) stops under Reduce Motion.
  • No movement of ours over a system surface.
