Area A. Capture: motion prototype

Each section plays one moment of MOTION.md on demand, keyed to the screen and state ids in DESIGN.md and to the design system v2 primitives (m.*, T-*) in docs/design/system/MOTION.md. The phones are simplified crops; layout and words belong to PROTOTYPE.html and WORDS.md. System surfaces (Apple's picker, native sheets and confirmations) are drawn as labelled stand-ins: their motion is Apple's, not ours. Glass is approximated with CSS blur; it materialises by its blur and tint, never by the element's opacity. The log beside each phone shows the timeline in milliseconds and every haptic (red) and screen reader event (blue); nothing in the log is shown to a parent. Fictional family only: Asha. Pictures are drawn shapes.
