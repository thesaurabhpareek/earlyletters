## What I am buying

One sentence: a symbol a tired parent feels before they read, that a child could draw on a birthday card in twenty years, and that sits beside Aesop and Apple Books without apologising.

The product truth the mark must hold (in priority order):
1. **Kept**: words held exactly, for years. Permanence, care, not decoration.
2. **Addressed**: from one person to one small person. Intimacy, direction (from, to).
3. **Spoken**: it begins as a voice. Nice to have in the mark; the name already says "Letters", so voice is the half the name does not carry, which is a reason to value it, but no microphone or soundwave.
4. **Time**: read later, years. Unhurried.

A mark that carries 1 and 2 cleanly beats a mark that crams in all four.

## Gates (pass or out, before scoring)

A concept that fails any gate is cut regardless of score.

- G1 No letter e, no EL monogram, no letterform as the symbol.
- G2 No banned motif: wax seal, quill, ribbon, sparkle, confetti, face, mascot, drawn baby, microphone, generic soundwave, speech bubble, rattle, footprint, stork, pram, flatline, drawn heart.
- G3 No horizontal bar across a form (strikethrough). No line that ends flat after movement (flatline).
- G4 No death, loss, grave, religious or political reading that a reasonable person in our 7 launch cultures would see in one glance. (A reading only a critic can construct is noted, not fatal.)
- G5 Survives 16px as a recognisable shape (not necessarily the full idea; the silhouette must hold).
- G6 Not a near copy of an existing well-known mark (Mailchimp, Gmail, Medium, Quora, Moon/Calm-like crescent marks, Day One, Notion, Substack, Airmail, Readwise, Kindle, Apple Books, Storyworth, Chatbooks). Close-but-different is a risk to record, not a gate.
- G7 Colours only from `packages/brand/index.ts`.

## Weighted criteria (100)

| # | Criterion | Weight | What 10 looks like | What 3 looks like |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | **Feeling in one second** (2am test) | 20 | Warmth and tenderness arrive before meaning. You want to touch it. | Neutral, corporate, or clever-first. |
| C2 | **Recall** (draw from memory in 5 seconds; describe in under 8 words) | 18 | A child could draw it: 1 to 3 strokes, one idea. | Needs specific curves remembered; describable only as "a sort of swoosh". |
| C3 | **Communicates without a caption** | 15 | Two of: letter, child, voice, keeping, read cold by a parent. | Requires the rationale paragraph. |
| C4 | **Elite / restraint** | 12 | Could be blind-embossed on a cloth book and look like it has always been there. No gimmick. | Looks like a template app icon or a children's brand. |
| C5 | **Distinctiveness and ownability** in baby, journal, memory, letters apps | 12 | Nothing in the category looks like it; defensible. | Envelope, open book, crescent, heart: category wallpaper. |
| C6 | **Scale and production** (16/29/40/60 px, emboss, single colour, light and dark, tinted iOS icon) | 10 | Same silhouette everywhere; a small optical cut exists and is honest. | Falls apart below 40px, or depends on fine contrast or thin lines. |
| C7 | **Craft** (curves, weight, optical balance, node economy, pairing with the Literata wordmark) | 8 | Tensioned curves, considered terminals, weight matches wordmark stems. | Lumpy beziers, even-weight rope, mismatched weight. |
| C8 | **System potential** (motion, illustration line, print, gift card) | 5 | Extends naturally into the CREATIVE.md single-line illustration system and the book cover. | A dead end; only works as a static icon. |

Score each 1 to 10, multiply by weight, divide by 10. Max 100.

## Tie breakers (in order)
1. Higher C1 + C2 combined. Feeling and memory beat everything else for a consumer product parents choose at 2am.
2. Fewer misreads in the culture critique.
3. Lower trademark risk.
4. Craft is fixable in v2; concept is not. Between a strong idea badly drawn and a weak idea well drawn, keep the strong idea.

## My known biases (to correct for)
- I like clever double readings. Parents do not reward cleverness; they reward warmth. Discount any concept whose best feature is a hidden second meaning that needs explaining (C3 catches this; also check C1 honestly).
- I like restraint to the point of emptiness. A plain circle is elite but says nothing. C3 and C5 must pull weight.
- I will be tempted by envelopes because the name says Letters. Envelopes are category wallpaper (Gmail, Mailchimp, every mail app). An envelope must earn its place with a distinct twist or it scores 4 or less on C5.
- Moons read as sleep apps (Calm-adjacent, Headspace Sleep, BabyCenter-ish nursery decor). Same discipline as envelopes.
- Critics' rankings are evidence, not votes. The parent panel (K1) is weighted most on C1; strategy (K2) and trademark (K3) on C5 and G6; culture (K4) on G4; craft (K5) on C7; production (K6) on C6. Where a specialist rules inside their specialty, I defer unless I can show the render contradicts them.

## How I will judge
- From renders I have looked at myself at 1x and magnified, never from SVG source or prose.
- Each concept scored before reading critiques, then reconciled after, with every change of score explained in the synthesis.
- Cut to at most 3. Keep a deliberate spread if quality allows (not three variations of the same idea), unless one direction clearly dominates.
