# Brand consistency audit

v0.1, 2026-10-03. Branch `feat/email-brand-library`. Read-only audit: nothing outside this file was changed.

**The standard audited against**
- Primary mark: `packages/brand/assets/logo/r3/final/final-a` (two quotation marks, large and small; EB Garamond W4 wordmark; "Leather T" sepia gradient tile `#9A613C` to `#7F4F30`, paper `#FBF8F3` mark; dark tile `#2C2926` to `#1F1B18`, `#D9A47E` mark). Its packaged successor is `packages/brand/assets/logo/primary` plus a registry, being built in parallel; fixes below say "primary" for that folder.
- Name and identity: `packages/brand/index.ts` only (CLAUDE.md).
- Colour, type, space: `packages/design-tokens/src/tokens.ts`; fonts Mukta (UI), Literata (reading), Tiro Devanagari Hindi (Devanagari reading); EB Garamond only inside outlined logo artwork.
- Voice: `packages/content/VOICE.md`, `BRAND.md`, content rules in CLAUDE.md.
- Product facts: `docs/agents/BRIEF-2026-10-03.md` (decisions 3, 5, 6, 9, 10 bear on copy).

Every finding below was checked by reading the file at the line given. Generated outputs (`apps/web/dist`, `packages/emails/out`, `supabase/templates/*.html`) are not listed separately; they follow their sources and must be regenerated after the source fix.

## 1. Findings

Severity: **high** = a customer sees the wrong mark, a wrong claim, or two contradictory statements; **medium** = visible drift or a second source of truth that will drift; **low** = internal, cosmetic or documentation only.
