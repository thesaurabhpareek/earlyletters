# Plus and your Apple subscription

Your first two letters are free. Plus is a membership that lets you keep adding letters, to every book, as many as you like. Every letter you keep stays yours to read, play and export, with or without Plus. You buy Plus, change it, cancel it and ask for refunds through Apple, using your Apple Account.

## What Plus adds

- **Keep adding letters** to every book, as many as you like.

That is all Plus does in this version.

## What is free, with or without Plus

- **Your first two letters.** They are kept, and always will be. The two are shared across all your books, not two for each book.
- **Starting a book**, for each of your children. A second book does not need Plus.
- **Reading, playing your recordings, Read together and export**, for every letter you have kept.
- **Speaking or typing a letter.** Plus is asked for only when you tap Keep on a third letter, and the letter is never lost.

## What counts as a letter

A letter counts when you keep it: spoken or typed, in the book or private, and a recording you keep without words.

These do not count: a quiet-day mark (**Not much today**), a letter you have not kept yet, a take you recorded again, changing a word, and putting a letter back from Recently deleted.

Deleting a letter does not give a free letter back. Deleting the app and installing it again on the same phone usually does not either, because the app remembers how many letters you have kept.

## When you keep a third letter

The app shows "Keep adding to {child}'s book". Your letter is safe on your phone whatever you choose.

- **See Plus** opens Apple's subscription screen.
- **Keep it here for now** keeps the letter on your phone, untouched. Tonight shows "A letter is waiting to be read back." Come back to it once Plus is on.
- **Redeem a code** opens Apple's sheet for an offer code.

Once Plus is on, the same letter is there to read back. Tap Keep, and it goes into the book. If you held more than one letter, keep each one the same way.

Plus needs the internet to start. If you are offline, your letter waits on your phone until then.

Plus is offered in the United States. It belongs to the Apple Account that buys it, and you do not need an Early Letters account to start it.

Plus can be added on iOS 17 or later.

## When you are charged

Plus renews on its own until you cancel. With a free trial, Apple charges you when the free trial ends. Without one, Apple charges you when you confirm. After that, Apple charges you at the start of each month or year.

While Plus is on, Settings, Plan shows when your free trial ends, or when your plan renews. During a free trial it also shows the last day to cancel so you pay nothing.

## How to cancel

Cancel any time, at least 24 hours before your free trial or your current month or year ends. Plus keeps working until then.

**In Early Letters:** Settings, Plan, **Manage subscription**. Apple's own subscription screen opens.

**On your iPhone:**

1. Open the Settings app.
2. Tap your name.
3. Tap Subscriptions.
4. Tap Early Letters.
5. Tap Cancel Subscription.

If there is no Cancel Subscription button, your plan is already cancelled and will not renew.

**Deleting the app does not cancel Plus.** Cancel with Apple as above.

## Refunds

Apple handles every refund for App Store purchases. We cannot give or promise a refund, and Apple decides each request.

**In Early Letters:** Settings, Plan, **Request a refund**. Apple's refund screen opens. This button shows for the person who bought Plus.

**On the web:**

1. Sign in to reportaproblem.apple.com with your Apple Account.
2. Tap "I'd like to", then choose "Request a refund".
3. Choose the reason, then choose Next.
4. Choose Early Letters Plus, then choose Submit.

You can check the status of your request on the same site.

A refund never changes your letters or recordings. If Apple gives a refund, Plus ends. Every letter you kept stays yours to read, play and export.

## Sharing Plus with your co-parent

> **Version 1.1.** Co-parent sharing is not in this version yet. Plus can still be shared through Apple Family Sharing, which is Apple's feature. Whether it needs anything from Early Letters at v1.0 is for `payments` to confirm before this section is published.

Plus can be shared through Apple Family Sharing. When you and your co-parent are in the same Family Sharing group, your co-parent gets Plus too, at no extra cost.

**If you have not set up Family Sharing:** open the Settings app, tap your name, tap Family, then follow the steps to invite your co-parent.

**To share your subscriptions with your family:** open the Settings app, tap your name, tap Subscriptions, tap Manage Family Access, then turn on Share Eligible Subscriptions.

Then your co-parent opens Early Letters. Settings, Plan says "Shared with you through Apple Family Sharing." If Plus does not show, tap **Restore purchases** in Settings, Plan.

If you are not in the same Family Sharing group, each of you has your own plan, and everything free stays free for both of you.

## A new phone

Plus comes with your Apple Account. Sign in to the same Apple Account on your new phone and open Early Letters. If Plus does not show, go to Settings, Plan and tap **Restore purchases**.

## If a payment does not go through

Plus stays on for a while, so you have time to update your payment details in your Apple Account. Settings, Plan says there is a problem with the payment. If Apple still cannot take the payment, Plus pauses until it can. Every letter you have kept is safe, and new letters wait on your phone.

## If Plus ends

Every letter and recording you kept stays yours to read, play and export, including Read together, in every book. Nothing you wrote changes.

To keep new letters, Plus can start again. Until then, a new letter waits on your phone, untouched, after you tap **Keep it here for now**.

## Price changes

We may change the price of Plus for new subscribers. While your subscription continues, you keep the price you already pay.

## Offer codes and gifts

If you have an offer code for Plus, go to Settings, Plan and tap **Redeem a code**. Apple's sheet opens. You can also tap **Redeem a code** when the app asks for Plus to keep a letter.

Gifts of Plus are not available yet.

## Questions

For charges, refunds and your Apple Account, Apple can help: reportaproblem.apple.com. For anything about Early Letters, write to us at {SUPPORT_EMAIL}. Apple handles payment for Plus. We never see your card details, and we never need them.

Our Subscription terms are part of our Terms of Service. Open them from Settings, **Terms of Service**, or from the links on Apple's subscription screen.
