# Roadmap, December 2026 to May 2027 (draft, 19 Nov 2026)

> SUPERSEDED-BY docs/product/prd/23-dependency-map-and-plan.md (parts only, see its section 8: the order of value table; keep its dates table) | 20 Nov 2026 | Rest of this file is unchanged | Registry: docs/product/prd/REGISTRY.md

Owner: the Chief Product Officer role, with Karma. Status: a proposal. Release dates and what is in each release are the founder's decisions; every window below is provisional until he sets it. It replaces the dates in `docs/backlog/FUTURE.md` and its 28 Oct re-baseline, which assumed staged server releases that no longer exist: the first store release already has sign-in and sync on (D-157, D-160).

Why the windows are months, not days: the pace is set by counted reviews and the founder's device time, and neither has been measured yet (`docs/product/prd/13-engineering-plan.md` section 1). After the first two waves we will know a rate and can date the rest.

## Capacity, honestly

FUTURE.md counted about 125 person-weeks wanted against about 72 held. That was a model of people, and the work is done by agent sessions whose limit is review. What we have not measured: counted reviews per day, device runs per build, the founder's hours per week. Until we have, this roadmap is an order, not a promise. `docs/ROADMAP.md` keeps its Superseded banner and should point here once this merges.
