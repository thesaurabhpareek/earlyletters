NX-36 Build 12 wireframes

Companion to NX-36-build-12-screen-specs.md (9 Nov 2026, draft). Static HTML and CSS, no scripts, no fonts, no images and no external requests. Colours are the real hex values from packages/design-tokens/src/tokens.ts (light: bg #FBF8F3, raised #FFFFFF, text #2B2722, muted #6B645B, accent #8A5A3B, soft #F1E6DC, line #E6DED3, controlBorder #8A8175, destructive #B5473A; dark: bg #161412, raised #201D1A, text #F2ECE4, muted #B3AA9E, accent #D9A47E, soft #3A2E25, line #33302C, controlBorder #857C70, destructive #F08C7C). Typefaces are stand-ins (Georgia for the letter serif, the system font for Mukta); the lamp is a soft stand-in, not tokens.atmosphere; icons are plain shapes. These are layout and hierarchy studies, not pixel specs. AX5 frames use an illustrative 2.4 scale, not a measured AX5 size, and show the whole scroll length. Copy is placeholder and needs content and founder approval. Three tabs only: Tonight, Book, You.
