## 3. Top three risks, each with a way back
1. **Planner restructure under every reminder user (B1, B8, B9).** One chooser now decides which of five sources fires, on top of a 60-in-total ceiling (plan notices first) shared with a second scheduler, back-off, a new anchor and a re-plan on five triggers. A wrong rank or a lost re-plan silently drops or doubles a note for everyone, not just for day users. Controls: the property test, the J-N4 days journey with an injected clock, `planSignature` stability test, `select` seam with the floor selector, a local bench (CI asserts counts and a generous bound, not 10 ms). Way back: B8 and B9 are behind `EXPO_PUBLIC_DAYS` for the day sources; B1 changes behaviour for existing kinds only where two notes shared a date (the card says that never happens, so a revert restores today's).
2. **The L4 days file (B5, B6).** If the exclusion read-back is skipped, if one session-end path misses the clear, or if the module is absent (Expo Go), a family's faith lands in an iPhone backup or reaches the next account. Controls: write refuses on a false read-back, one hook on the user id (reuse of `session.ts`), the import-boundary and canary tests, the erase scan, DV-1 transfer row. Way back: the pause hides the feature; Erase everything removes the file; flag off.
3. **The lock-screen render and iOS behaviour nobody has seen (B7, DV-1).** Whether a hidden title shows on Apple Watch, Notification Summary and CarPlay, whether `previewPlaceholder` maps as expected, and whether `active` needs no entitlement are Unverified (`11-foundations.md:367`; `21:451`). A mistake outs a family's faith or a child's name on a lock screen. Controls: neutral title for every non-allowlisted day, App lock forces neutral, no category sets `hiddenPreviewsShowTitle`, no string holds a day name and a child name, a four-switch snapshot test. Way back: `killSwitches.libraryDays` cancels pending notes at the next re-plan; flag stays off until DV-1 passes.
Honourable fourth: level 2's weak-linked Swift and a launch crash below iOS 17.2 (P3, DV-8); mitigated by the `#available` guard, the podspec platform fix and the device row; the flag stays off if there is no phone.
Other risks: R-1 slip inserted after first paint moves Speak (file 15 section 1.3); R-2 the plan-notice scheduler needs a re-plan hook owned by `payments`; R-3 `Intl` time-zone support on Hermes (file 13 section 3, Assumption).
