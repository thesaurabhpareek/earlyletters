## Update Sat 15 Nov: review fixes (lenses A and B at 5e896fed)
Both reviews returned CHANGES on one defect: the route held "Opening your book" only for a ready, sync-on session. For `checkingConsent` (relaunch while the consent gate is read), for `ready` with sync `unknown` (the gate could not be read: offline at relaunch, no answer) and during `signingOut` it passed `newPhone: 'new'`, so the gate fell to the child step, the promise step mounted with Continue live, `pastWelcome` latched, and when the connection returned the engine pulled the account's book and a new local book was queued under it. Fixed:
- **The hold covers every session whose sync is not a definite off** (`gate.logic.ts heldNewPhoneView`, `use-first-run-route.ts`): `checkingConsent`, and `signingOut` with sync `on` or `unknown`, are the loading screen (heading only); `ready` with sync `unknown` is the slow screen, or the connection screen offline, where Try again re-reads the consent gate through the provider's `refreshGate` and Try another way signs out as before. The account question and the 10 s clock still start only at `ready` + sync `on`. A session that has said no to sync (`needsConsent`, `ready`/`signingOut` with sync `off`) keeps the earlier route: `machine.logic.ts` gives it no sync (`canSync` false; `SIGN_OUT_REQUESTED` from it carries `sync: 'off'`), so nothing can pull a book to it and the consent sheet or the local start is the designed path. The hold never applies over a book on the phone, and nothing is read with the switch off.
- **"Your letters are coming across." only once the account has said it holds a book** (lens B M1): `useNewPhone` exposes `hasBook`, the route `accountHasBook`, and `OpeningBookScreen` takes `lettersComing`; the heading stands alone while asking, so an account with no letters (a new account relaunched on the child step, a returning account whose book is gone) never reads it.
- **Device row S1-204**: (h) was not runnable (Erase everything is not offered on a phone that has signed in: `erase.tsx localDeletionAllowed()`, `store.ts syncOwner(db) === null`, the owner written at claim and kept once any row landed); it is now a fresh install, with the store function's clearing left to `use-new-phone-hook.test.ts`. (j) adds the relaunch with airplane mode on: the connection screen, never the child step, no Continue to tap. (a), (b) and (f) say what the code does: the body's timing, "Opening your book." alone for R, and VoiceOver reaching the arrival line after the cover band (not first). The same two comments softened in `onboarding.tsx` and `book.tsx`.
- **Tests**: the pin at `use-first-run-route.test.ts` that asserted the gap now asserts the hold (sync off stays the child step); six route-hook tests (checkingConsent held with no body, ready/unknown online with Try again re-reading the gate, ready/unknown offline, signingOut held for sync on and unknown, the body only after `hasBook` and gone after Try again); a pure `heldNewPhoneView` table in `new-phone-route.test.ts`; a hook `hasBook` test; and a pin for the arrival write in `onboarding.tsx` (lens A 4: it had none). Red on the old source: 10 of the 10 new or changed behaviour tests failed with `apps/mobile/src` reverted to 5e896fed. Mutations caught: the write removed (1 test), the signingOut hold narrowed to sync on (2), Try again never re-reading the gate (1), the hold applied over a local book (1), the body always shown (1).
- **Not done, and why**: lens A 3 (the consent sheet's own `readAccountBooks` and its "No book here yet" over the route's). The sheet's read runs only for trigger `sign_in`, no local book, `ready`/`on`, which is exactly where the route now asks, so the second read and the double Start a book remain. Skipping it means removing the sheet's `noBook` branch (dead afterwards) and rewriting four source pins in `test/first-run-new-account.test.ts:219-227` from #737; left for a follow-up with the #737 pins, or Karma's call.
