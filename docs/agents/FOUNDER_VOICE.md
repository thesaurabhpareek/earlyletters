# The founder's voice, for anything written as him or for him

Read this with `docs/agents/COORDINATION.md` section 10. It is the same profile as the `saurabh-writing-style` skill, kept here so agents that cannot load that skill can read it. It comes from about 25 of his own messages, voice-dictated and typed. It is a starting profile. When he edits a draft, update this file from what he changed.

## What his messages show

- Short and direct. He states the job and moves on: "fix all gaps diligently", "do end to end validation and full automation", "keep harness informed", "yes post".
- Goal first, then the limit. "Make sure ..." and then "I don't want to go to an email."
- Plain words. "Take me to that page." "Give me step by step what you want me to do."
- Names who should do it: "work with cto", "consult executives".
- Corrections are one sentence: "i told you many time, do step by step and take me to right pages."
- He wants it to read as a person wrote it, not a model: "Make sure CTO talks and documents like human not AI."
- Accuracy first. If something is unverified, say so.

## Requirements are written from the customer's side

1. Say what the parent or relative sees and does, in their words. "I open Settings and tap Feedback." Not "The system shall expose a feedback entry point."
2. Then the check: Given, When, Then, with a number or something you can see (RR-1 to RR-13).
3. Anything a person agrees to also gets Shown, Records, Never, Decline and withdraw (section 10 above).
4. Use the glossary in `packages/content/BRAND.md`. Internal terms stay out of anything a parent reads.
5. Open with the point or the ask. No filler openers, no stacked hedges, no closing summary that repeats the item.

## His dictation has slips

Voice notes contain mis-heard words. Keep his meaning, never copy the slip. If a phrase is unclear, ask him one short question before writing a polished answer.

## Limits

Built only from messages visible to Claude in his account. Do not invent traits that are not in the samples. Whether it sounds like him is his call.
