## 0. The short version

I traced every obligation a person or a store has toward a public, servers-on, account-first Build 12 (42 items, section 2). Most are already written down somewhere. The problem is where: the rows sit in BL-757 to BL-774 (PR #684) and BL-775 to BL-815 (PR #707), neither on `develop`, and most of the ones that matter for a store submission are `blocked` or `needs-decision`, with no pointer from a Build 12 gate. Eleven things have no requirement, no row, or no gate. They are NX-38-R1 to R11.

| Class | Count | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Required by the cut | 8 (R1, R2, R3, R6, R7, R8, R9, R11 in part) | A store rule, a law or the founder's own cut already decided makes it part of Build 12. No scope is added; a row and a test were missing |
| Founder decision | 5 (R4c, R5, R10, R11 Plus line, and the gate additions) | It would add work or spend beyond the cut, or it chooses between two readings. I give a recommendation and do not decide |
| Wait | listed in section 2 | Can ship in 1.0.1 or later, with the reason |

Four findings the founder should hear first:

1. **Untrue words are in the build today (EVIDENCE).** The Delete account page says it removes "recordings, photos" from our servers; sign-in and Account text says "{child}'s other parent can write in the same book"; the store caption says "Your book stays on your phone." In Build 12 there are no photos, recordings stay on the phone, co-parent is hidden, and letter text syncs. A claims test exists but treats co-parent as true whenever servers are on (R1, R5).
2. **The G2 gate names "five addresses" and no list of them exists.** On 8 Nov `/support`, `/delete-account` and `/subscription-terms` answered 404 and `/privacy` said the app is not out (gap map 1.11). Nothing makes a script check the list (R2).
3. **The local-start fallback has no row that finishes it.** BL-783 draws the button, BL-784 the card, BL-803 specifies the merge-back rule. No row builds the claim (account already has a book, local book has kept letters). A fallback that cannot end is a data-loss and a promise problem (R6).
4. **BL id collision (process).** PR #707 defines BL-775 to BL-783 again. `develop` already has BL-775 to BL-783 (merged in #715, c350be3e: Erase everything follow-ups). Section 7.
